## A Promise in waitForFunction never waits (2026-11-14)

Playwright's waitForFunction polls until the function returns something truthy. A Promise is always truthy, so a check that is async, or is `fetch(...).then(...)`, ends the wait on the first look: one that could never hold resolved after 36 ms of a 1.5 s timeout. 46 waits in the panel test were like that, and two of them were guarding something that was not true (a drag that never reached the canvas, a slider whose top is 1.991 of 2).

What we do now: a check that fetches or awaits goes through `pollFor()` in tests/ui/poll.js, which polls one page.evaluate at a time. tests/test_ui_waits.py fails on any waitForFunction whose check can return a Promise, however it is written. When a wait is added, make it fail once on purpose and see it time out. And do not name a shared helper after a word the file already uses: a local `until` quietly took the place of the imported one.
