## Read a state after the thing that makes it, not after the call that asked for it (2026-11-07)

Four tests failed once each because they read a result at a fixed moment: right after a request that another thread finishes (Off, then the player's redraw), right after a state word that comes before the data (playing, then the reader thread's bytes), a rate taken once and compared with another taken later on a machine whose load changed, and a click raced against a poll that redraws the thing being clicked. What we do now: wait for the condition itself with a deadline (the test still fails if it never comes), take two measurements that are compared with each other in turn and not one after the other, and change the page's inputs only after the action that needs them. An unawaited promise is also a way to lose a run's cleanup: await it together with the action that can fail.
