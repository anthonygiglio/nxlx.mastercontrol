## Addendum to D106 (2026-10-24): as built, step (e), the panel's controls for a source's chain, and the review of #172

**As built: step (e), the panel's controls for a source's chain** (the panel only; pvj-render is as step d left it, 0.7.0).

- **Where:** the Sources card of Projection mapping, on the New renderer only (the box's `GET /api/effects/chain` says `available`; on classic the card is as it was, and the test checks no chain control shows there).
- **Each source's row:** on Sources 2 and up, a **Shader** row (a choice of the library's generators and Play, which plays it to that source as a shader pad would: step d), shown while the box names slot 0 among the source's slots and Shaders is on; the shader playing there is the one chosen, read from slot 0 of the chain (slot 0 is the shader that is the source's picture, never shown as an effect). Then an **Effect** row (slot 1: the effect, On or Change, Off, and its amount while on), shown while Effects is on. Source 1's first effect stays under Effect, as D74 has it, and its shader the Shaders screen's.
- **Advanced:** slots 2 and 3 ("More effects (advanced)", and on Source 1 "More effects after the Effect") sit in a closed section, open where one of them is on: D74 had one effect, and the owner sees the rest only by asking for it. Logged as a change for him to see.
- **The two items of #170's review logged for this step:** the effects' cost estimate now counts every pass (a shader of several passes runs its whole text once a pass, its buffer reads included: reads and rounds times the passes); and a play asks once whether the player runs programs (the parse, its cache and the text from that one answer, on Source 1's shaders and the effects: `_parsed(path, native)`, `show(..., native=)`).
- **The item of #171's review logged for this step:** the shaders that are the picture of Sources 2 to N are built again, with the values and controls they have, when an upload replaces their file and when Picture detail changes (`Chains.rebuild_generators`); one the renderer refuses then leaves the one before it on, and the log says so.
- **Tests:** the panel (tests/ui/panel.test.js): no chain control on classic; each source's effect, its amount, Off, another On; the advanced slots open where one is on; slot 0 shown as the source's shader and never as an effect slot; a shader played to a source; the bodies sent. `test_the_cost_of_an_effect_counts_every_pass`, `test_a_play_asks_once_whether_the_player_runs_programs`, `test_a_replaced_upload_and_a_change_of_picture_detail_build_source_2_again`. Six break-checks, each caught (the journal).
- **Not built:** a source's shader values and controls in the card (the API takes them: step d), a shader or effects on a controller, Vibes on Sources 2 to N.

**The review of #172 (2026-10-24).** Two Medium, two Low-Medium and five Low, fixed on the same branch:

- **Medium: a rebuild could undo what came to the source meanwhile.** `rebuild_generators` took its list, let the lock go, and built each shader again: a Stop, a clip or another shader that landed on that source in between was put back under the old shader (confirmed on the real renderer). Each rebuild now hands in the program file the source held when it was asked (`expect`), and under the chains' lock, before the renderer is asked, a source whose file is no longer that one is left as it is. Test: `test_a_rebuild_leaves_what_came_to_the_source_meanwhile` (the reviewer's probe: the parse is hooked, and a Stop, a clip, another shader land between).
- **Medium: "More effects (advanced)" closed at every redraw** (4 s). What is opened or closed by hand is kept in the card over its redraws; where nothing was done by hand it opens where one of its effects is on.
- **Low-Medium: the chain rows at 390 px.** The advanced row's choice was squeezed to 20 px and the row ran past the card: the card's rows wrap, a choice keeps at least 9 em, an amount 6 em. The panel test sweeps the rows at 390, 820 and 1440 px (nothing past the card, no choice under 120 px).
- **Low-Medium: the cost of passes counted twice.** The text's count already sums both PASSINDEX branches, so times the passes a two-pass blur of 17 taps counted 68 and was refused at upload. The count stays the upload's limit (34 there); only the weight takes it times the passes. Test with that blur.
- **Low:** a rebuild drops a value whose input the new file no longer has (or has as another kind) and builds with the rest; the answer of an upload or a Picture detail change names the sources it could not build again (`sources_not_rebuilt`), and the Shaders page says so; the Sources card asks for the chains only where there are sources to hold them (none on classic); the libraries are asked again after a minute, or after 10 s when a chain names one they lack; a choice made by hand stays chosen over the redraws until it is put on, and an amount being dragged is not drawn over; the same Picture detail again builds nothing; an effect put on asks once whether the player runs programs (test beside: reverting it was caught by nothing); the panel test waits on what is sent, never on a fixed pause.
- **Logged, not fixed (older than this step):** the effects' count does not count a read of a buffer with IMG_THIS_PIXEL, and IMG_NORM_PIXEL or IMG_PIXEL of a buffer stops it with "its brackets do not match", so such an upload is refused as uncountable; and an upload that replaces an effect does not build again the chain's slots 1 to 3 that hold it (Source 1's effect is the engine's own).
- Twelve break-checks, each caught (the journal).
