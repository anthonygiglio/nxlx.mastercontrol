## Addendum to D134 (2026-11-18): flashing filters need the opt-in everywhere but the hold (J-128)

Rule 7 said the Effects page could still put the Strobe on, steady. That is closed. A filter that is in `HOLD_ONLY` or whose file says it flashes is now started only by the hold (mctl/strobe.py) or while "Allow faster than the flash limit" is on. This covers the Effects list (`POST /api/effects` with an id), presets of another effect, the chain routes, Next and Previous, and the controllers' one button and OSC on and off (they step the same list, which leaves such filters out while the opt-in is off). One rule, `Effects.flash_gate`, on the node, not in the panel. Refused with 409 and a plain sentence; with the opt-in on the answer carries a seizure warning, and the panel disables "Put on" with a line while it is off. Why: the node must not rely on the panel, and a steady strobe from a list is the case the hold exists to prevent. Also: a lit chain slot cannot be raised from amount 0 through the chain routes (the hold's own call is not asked); switching the opt-in off takes off a flashing filter that is on, and one in a chain slot, but not the Strobe's parked copy or one a hold lights; the effect that was on before a hold is not put back if it flashes and the opt-in is off; a file replaced by one that says it flashes goes off; a wish queued by id is checked again when the worker runs it. Not covered, as before: what an effect does to a fast clip (the node cannot see it). New: "says it flashes" is words only (a CATEGORIES or DESCRIPTION that says so, shaders.py), as D134 already said for the Strobe's neighbours; a flashing file that does not say so is not caught. Who may switch the opt-in: today `POST /api/shaders` with action config is the Operator's (`tests/test_roles.py`), while SHADERS.md and the manual say full access; the wording of the refusals and the panel no longer says who.
