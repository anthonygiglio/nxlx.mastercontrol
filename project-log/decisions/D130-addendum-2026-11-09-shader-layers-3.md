## Addendum to D130 (2026-11-09): slice 3, the panel takes the fade, and the build no longer holds the layers

Slice 3 makes shader pads use the Mix transition on the New renderer. Points settled in the build, each a change of behaviour on purpose:

1. **No carrier on the New renderer.** When the player says `layer-generators` and `program-fades` (`Player.fades_native()`), a shader is the front layer's picture and no blank clip is loaded under it. A cut into a shader (`Player.play_source`) loads the program into the front layer's generator and stops the clip that was there; a fade (`Player.fade_to_program`) builds it into the idle layer. Classic mpv, the Pi 3, a renderer from before 0.10.0 and a live input's pipe keep the carrier and D71's still, as D73 and D98 say. Because the panel's checks of "is a shader what plays" read mpv's `path` against the carrier's name (`is_carrier`, `Engine.on_screen`, Vibes, the status, the effect's size), the player gives that name back as a stand-in for `path` while a program is the picture and its layer is idle (`Player.path_now`, and `Player.status()["path"]`). This keeps those checks as they were, at the cost of a name that is not an mpv path.
2. **What fades.** `Api._shader_fade` answers (seconds, style) when the Mix is a crossfade, wipe or slide, the renderer draws that style, something is on the screen, and nothing is dark (no Blackout, no Fade out, opacity above 0). A shader chosen by hand or by a pad (`LiveEngine.play`, `Engine.show` with `cut` True) then fades from a clip or from another shader. The same shader shown again (a slider) is exchanged in place, not faded. Vibes (`cut` False) still dips, as D130 section 9 question 3 decided: it waits for slice 4 and a soak. `Api._layered_fade` is true for anything `fades_native()` allows, so a clip after a shader is a fade too. A dip chosen in the Mix still cuts into a shader, as before.
3. **A refusal in a fade.** The GPU's words come back from `pvj-fade-to-program`; the screen is unchanged (a fade that was running has ended on its incoming side, the documented deviation of slice 2) and the tap says "The shader before it stays on." or "The picture before it stays on." with the words. A fade that fails for another reason (the renderer would not draw the style, a lost answer) is a cut into the shader, logged.
4. **A value refused during a shader to clip fade** (slot 0, "invalid parameter", while the outgoing generator still draws) is expected: `Player.native_values` answers False and the worker does not show an error.
5. **A lost answer from `pvj-fade-to-program`.** The player asks the renderer which program source 1's generator holds. If it is the one asked for, the fade is recorded as taken; if it is not, or the renderer cannot say, `_native_loaded[0]` is cleared so the next push or value loads this side's source again. The lists are touched before a fade as a crossfade touches them.
6. **The build no longer holds `layer_lock` (the medium finding of the slice 2 review).** `pvj-fade-to-program` now lets go of `layer_lock` while the render thread builds the program (up to 3 s). A Stop, a load, a step in a list, a level or a crossfade from another client is served at once, inside the player's 2 s timeout. `building` (under `layer_lock`) names the layer being built into; `end_fade` (which every command that changes what the front plays calls) ends the build too: it asks the program off and sets `building` to -1. When the fade command takes the lock again it finds that, answers "the layer was ended before the load took effect" and starts no fade. A level, a get and a set do not end the build. `fx_lock` stays held, so loads and clears still go one at a time. Lock order is unchanged (`fx_lock`, `layer_lock`, `app.lock`). The panel's call is answered "the layer was ended" and shows nothing as an error.
7. **The Mix card** says on a node that cuts into a shader (classic, the Pi 3, an older renderer) that a shader pad still arrives with a cut (`mix.shader_cut`).

Tests: `ProgramBuildDoesNotHoldTheLayersTest` (slow build, a Stop from a second client with the player's 2 s timeout), `TwoClientStressTest` (the review's stress, bounded), `PlayerShaderLayerTest` and `EngineFadeTest` in `tests/test_native_shaders.py`. The D73 tests of tests/test_shader_pads.py that pin a cut into a shader stay as they are: they run over the classic stand-in, which is the Pi 3. Device rows LT5 and LT6 are written down, not run.
