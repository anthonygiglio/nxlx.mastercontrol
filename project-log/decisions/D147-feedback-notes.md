## D147. Feedback notes from the Room screen: any signed-in device sends, the Owner reads (2026-11-22)

A **Feedback** button on the Room screen opens a sheet over it: three big faces (good, so-so, bad), then an optional text of up to 1000 characters, then Send; it answers "Thanks, sent" and closes. A face alone is a whole note. It is for alpha testers at a venue, who are casual and not technical, so it must take under 30 seconds (the owner's decision on J-179, slice A). The node adds what the tester never types: its name and id, the screen that was open, the time, what plays on each source (Source 1 from the public status, the others from the Sources list) and the sender's role (owner, operator or guest). A note keeps no device name, no device id, no address and no token.

Who: `POST /api/feedback` is at the lowest level (any paired device), so a Guest may send, also while guest controls are locked (feedback is not a control of the room, so the lock does not apply). It is **not** in policy.GUEST and not in LEGACY_LIVE: a Guest gains no other form of any route, and the box's own callers (MIDI, OSC, DMX, a Room scene) and a support session below full cannot send. Through remote support nobody sends (support.REMOTE_DENY): a note is from someone in the room. `GET /api/feedback` is the Owner's, and a support session of the Owner's role may read it as it may read the event log (D84, D125; the same reason: it ends with the session and names no person or device). The words are scrubbed of every secret the node knows, as the event log's text is. This is consistent with D99, which keeps from remote support what changes the room and outlives the session; reading a note changes nothing.

Storage follows the event log (D125): `feedback.json` in the panel's state folder, written by the panel's own account by a rename from a temporary name opened with O_NOFOLLOW, mode 0640, nothing root-owned, and read with O_NOFOLLOW, so a link planted at either name is not followed. The file is UTF-8 (not escaped: an emoji is 4 bytes, not 12) and kept under 2 MB, the oldest notes dropped first; it is read only when it is a regular file under that size (opened with O_NONBLOCK as well, so a pipe planted at the name cannot hold the panel). The newest 500 notes are kept. A send is limited to one in five seconds for each paired device and, for a Guest, by the guest limit on actions (10 in 10 seconds a device, 30 for all guests, counted apart from the controls' and without the lock); and the node as a whole takes at most 30 notes in ten minutes and 200 in a day, so a crowd of devices cannot push the real notes out. A refused send is a 429 with `retry_after` (for the node's share: "Lots of feedback just now, try again in a few minutes"); a failed write or a refused send stores nothing and starts no wait. What plays is kept in plain words: an NDI source is only "an NDI source" (its name carries the sender's machine name), a clip by its file name, a stream by its saved name or "a stream", and the words are scrubbed of every secret the node knows when kept and again when read. On the read-only root the notes are lost at a restart and the Owner is told so, as for events.

Cost / rules out: no page of notes for anyone but the Owner (the Owner's list is a card under What happened on Setup > Health, with a download of the whole list as a file); no reply to the sender; no sending from a controller or through support; no way to keep more than the newest 500 notes or 2 MB. The rate limits are in memory, so a restart of the panel forgets them. A note's text is the sender's own words and is shown as text only.
