## D129. Text and logos are "graphics": drawn by the node, decoded only by a sandboxed rasteriser, beside the masks, not instead of them (2026-11-08, decided)

The owner's words (2026-10-30): "We should include a Text Generator source." and "I want to be able to upload images, DJ logos for example, use them as a source or overlay, and apply some effects or keying to the image." On 2026-11-08 he chose to build both into Mastercontrol, not to stream HTML over NDI from a laptop (that test reached about 24 fps, ran 0.8 s behind and loaded the laptop heavily). Full design: design/text-and-logo-sources_2026-11-08.md. Decided: on 2026-11-08 Anthony accepted all five recommended answers: (1) a new Play > Graphics screen with the masks kept separate; (2) a Guest may not push text, Operator and Owner only; (3) bundled open-licence fonts only for now, font upload later and Owner only; (4) an SVG logo is turned into a PNG and the SVG is not kept; (5) As Source N may use a source slot, and Over the screen is the default.

What it is: a graphic is a text or a logo, up to 8, in a new settings key "graphics" (no schema bump). Shown over the screen, over a source, or as a source (D101). Drawn by pvj-render's GPU on Pi 4, Pi 5 and desktop (fade, typewriter, scroll and crawl per frame; alpha, luma and chroma keys; up to 3 effects as in D106); drawn by mpv overlay-add on the Pi 3 and on classic (static, stepped fade and typewriter, keys and colour effects baked in). Text and logos are made into pictures by a new small unprivileged program, pvj-raster (FreeType, libpng, libjpeg-turbo, a vendored SVG rasteriser), one process per job, no network, memory and time limits. Uploads accept PNG, JPEG and SVG by magic bytes, are normalised to a clean PNG, and the SVG is not kept. Fonts are bundled, open licence only; uploading fonts is deferred and would be Owner only. Operators and the Owner edit; a Guest does not. OSC, MIDI and TouchOSC reach it (text pushed by one OSC string). The mask list of D100 stays as it is.

Why: the nodes already have the pieces (sources, effect chains, an overlay path, an upload path), a browser-in-the-loop cannot meet 60 Hz and low delay, and the one risky part, decoding files from phones, can be isolated. Cost / rules out: a new C program to build and review; no emoji, right-to-left or CJK text at first; SVG text and filters are not supported; a graphic as a source takes a source slot (a Pi 4 has two); the Pi 3 gets fewer motions. Needs an independent review of the upload pipeline before the drawing slices build on it.
