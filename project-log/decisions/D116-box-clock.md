## D116. One box clock: a flywheel fed by tap, OSC and sound, not a new beat tracker (2026-10-31)

The box now has one clock (`pvj/clock.py`). Once it has a tempo it keeps a tempo and a beat position by itself, through gaps in whatever feeds it. Its sources, most trusted first, are `link` (a slot only: nothing feeds it yet), `tap` (taps, or a tempo typed in), `osc` (the existing `/nxlx/sound/in/beat` and `/bpm`) and `sound` (pvj-sound's tempo and beats). **The hand wins (the lead's decision):** a tap or a tempo set by number holds until `clear`, and sound never takes over from it; the status still shows how old the last tap is. OSC and sound are machines and keep short holds (5 s and 3 s); a quiet machine hands over to the next, and when all are quiet the last good tempo keeps running and the status says which source steered it last and how old its word is. A Link source, when it arrives, will outrank the hand. Sound is believed only at a confidence of 0.5 or more, and then only to set the tempo and to push the phase gently (a hit further than 0.3 beat from the grid is ignored, a nearer one moves the phase by 15 percent of its distance), so a stray hit cannot jump the beat. A tap or an OSC beat sets the phase at once. Taps are averaged (2 to 8, a pause over 2 seconds starts again, 30 to 300 BPM or refused); a first tap with no tempo anywhere sets nothing, so a tempo needs two taps. The clock has no thread: the panel's once-a-second tick moves it on, and whoever asks for a value does too.

Consumer: while a tap or OSC steers the clock, the levels record that the panel's meter, OSC out (`/nxlx/sound/pulse`, `bpm`, `beat`) and the classic renderer's shader input read has the clock's pulse, tempo and beat count in place of sound's, with or without a sound device (the Owner's sound switch is still the Owner's; with no device the levels are zero and nothing is heard). **With sound alone nothing is changed: the beat is the service's own, and when the music stops the beat stops, as before.** The clock follows sound only so that it can hand over. The OSC beat message is sent only for a count that went up under the same supplier; a change of supplier or a count that went down is a silent resync, and a backwards nudge cannot beat twice. The levels file and pvj-render are not changed. The tap is one action, `clock_tap`, for MIDI and OSC (`/pvj/clock/tap`). There is no DMX channel for it: a tenth channel would be armed on any console that sends ten, so a neighbouring fixture would tap the clock. The routes are `GET /api/tempo`, `POST /api/tempo` and `POST /api/tempo/tap`; a support session may look and may not change.

Why: the owner wants the under-the-hood work wrapped up so he can rework the UI next, and the beat was the one thing that stopped between onsets. **Why not a new beat tracker:** the node's CPU is already near 80 percent, nobody has benchmarked the options on a Pi 4, and live tempo tracking is unreliable at best on real music. A flywheel that a person or a program can correct costs a few microseconds a call and fails in a way a person can fix with one tap. The user still chooses the audio source for sound reactivity (h30): the clock listens to nothing of its own; OSC beats reach it only through the sound source "Levels sent in over OSC", from the addresses the Owner listed, as before.

**Next:** Ableton Link comes next, into the `link` slot (`Clock.hear("link", bpm)`). MIDI clock in waits for a device to test with. The tempo is not saved: it starts empty after a restart, because saving it would have meant widening the sound setting's strict form and its tests; one tap, or the first confident sound or OSC tempo, starts it.

Cost / rules out: because the hand wins, a tapped tempo stays after the music changes until someone taps again or clears it. A person tapping while Link steers does nothing. OSC beats and tempos still need the Owner to choose "Levels sent in over OSC" as the sound source and to list the sender. The beat message over OSC counts from the clock's origin, not pvj-sound's own count. DMX cannot tap. Nothing here has met a real tapper, a Max patch or a band; `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md` BC1 to BC5 are not yet run on a node.
