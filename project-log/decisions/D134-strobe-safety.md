## D134. The Strobe is a lease, capped in three places, and spaced by a gap (2026-11-11)

What: a built-in filter `fx-strobe.fs` (rate, colour, flash length, flash to the colour or to black or the colour added) and a hold-to-strobe that stops the moment it is let go (`pvj/strobe.py`; `GET` and `POST /api/strobe`, `POST /api/strobe/hold`, `/renew`, `/release`; the MIDI actions `strobe_hold` and `strobe_rate`; the OSC addresses `/pvj/strobe` and its rate, colour, flash and way). The owner asked for "a Strobe effect, with rate and colour controls, plus a hold-to-strobe button for the panel, MIDI and OSC that stops the moment you let go". The panel button is the panel session's, on top of this API.

The rules, each with its reason:

1. **The cap is 3 flashes a second and sits in three places.** The filter caps its rate at 3 in its own code, counted in TIME (so no route can exceed it, whatever the input says; the GPU test gives it 99 and counts); the node sends at most 3 and a speed of 1; and the existing speed limit for a flashing filter (the flash limit, SHADERS.md) keeps the speed at 1 unless the owner's "Allow faster than the flash limit" is on. With it on, a rate above 3 is the filter's 3 times a speed above 1, to 12. Why: the photosensitive guideline is 3 a second, and the node must not rely on the panel to keep it.
2. **A hold is a lease, not a switch.** Each holder has a deadline; the node's watcher ends the strobe when the last has run out. The panel renews every 0.2 seconds and a lease from the panel lasts 0.5 seconds (so it ends within about 0.55 seconds of the last renewal); OSC is the same (the owner's decision, 2026-11-11: "OSC strobe must repeat while held"): the sender repeats `/pvj/strobe 1` about every 0.2 seconds, and one press with no repeats lasts at most about half a second. A MIDI pad sends a press and a release and nothing between, so the MIDI hub renews while the control is down and the controller is still read; its 10 second lease is only a backstop. No hold lasts more than 60 seconds in all, renewed or not. A renewal of a lease that has ended is refused (409), so a late message from a stalled network cannot start it again. Why: a closed tab, a dropped network, a pulled cable or a finger left on a button must not leave a strobe running in a room.
3. **Holders are counted, one lease each, up to 8**, and not one at a time. Why: with one holder a second person's press takes the first one's and the first one's release stops the second; with a lease for each, a holder that vanishes loses only its own and nothing can keep the strobe on after its holder is gone.
4. **Lit stretches are spaced by a gap of 0.35 seconds** (the effect worker's own gap; none with the opt-in). A change of the rate or the flash length while it flashes moves the filter's place in its cycle at once (it computes the place from TIME times the rate) and can start a flash early, and letting go and pressing again, as fast as a finger can, starts a flash at an arbitrary place each time; both would put flashes closer together than the rate says. So each ends the lit stretch and the next one starts 0.35 seconds after the last ended. A colour or a way changes at once. A test of random presses, releases, renewals and changes over thousands of steps checks it.
5. **The fast path is a loaded copy gated by its amount.** On the New renderer the strobe sits in a chain slot of source 1 (slot 3, else 2) at amount 0 and a hold is one value to the renderer: no file, no compile, none of the effect worker's gap. Without a chain, or with both slots taken, the strobe is put on as the effect and what was on is put back at the release (a compile each way; said in `GET /api/strobe` as `path: effect`).
6. **Who.** The Operator and the Owner (the route's minimum is "live"), and the controllers MIDI and OSC (the routes are in `policy.LEGACY_LIVE`, with a comment, as /api/graphics is). Never a Guest, with guest controls open or locked: a flash in the room is not something guest controls open. Remote support is refused the routes that start or shape a flash and may release (`support.REMOTE_DENY`): a helper cannot see the room, and a tunnel's delay is no dead-man.
7. **In the library, flagged flashing, never stepped onto.** `fx-strobe.fs` is listed, marked `flashes`, with `speed_max` 1 (4 with the opt-in), but it is not in the order Previous and Next step through (`effects.HOLD_ONLY`), so a controller cannot land on a steady strobe by stepping. Vibes plays generators only, so it is never in the rotation. The Effects page can still put it on, steady, at the cap, by the effect worker's rules.

Cost / rules out: the first hold after a restart, or after the loaded copy was taken off (5 minutes after its last use, or by a Stop), is a build and is not fast; the panel should send `arm` when its button is shown. A change of rate while it flashes blanks the strobe for 0.35 seconds (a knob ridden live is a staircase of stretches, not a smooth slide). Releasing waits for the player's own lock like every change of the picture, so a clip being loaded delays a stop. An OSC sender must repeat its 1 while held, which a TouchOSC button needs a small script for. What stays open, as for every effect: Amount ridden up and down through the Effects routes is a strobe played by hand, and a strobe put on from the Effects page is under the effect worker's rules, not the lease. Settings (rate, colour, flash, way) are in memory only. Not run on a board, not pressed by a person at a button, not measured on a Pi 4 (`UNMEASURED` in `pvj/effects.py`).
