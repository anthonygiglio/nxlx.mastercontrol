## Addendum to D129 (2026-11-09): what slice 3 changed from the design while it was built

Built as the design says (the GPU stage, texture upload, a quad, opacity, fade, typewriter, scroll and crawl over the screen after the final pass, the stepped fade and typewriter on classic and lean), with these differences, each for a reason found in the code:

- **Insertion point.** In `final_pass` (`render/src/main.c`), after the picture (the mapping's shader or the plain scale) and before the text layer. The masks (30) and the pairing codes (40 to 43) are drawn by the text layer and so stay on top; the captures and screenshots show the graphics. *Over Source N* and *As Source N* (slice 5) are still to place: they are in the source's own texture, not here.
- **The typewriter reveals along the ink, not per letter.** The design has the rasteriser return the right edge of each letter. That changes a reviewed, sandboxed program's output for one motion. Instead the picture is cut into as many equal bands as it has lines, each band's ink is measured when the picture is loaded (on the client's thread in the renderer, in Python on the classic path), and the lines are revealed one after the other at one pace along the ink with a soft edge. Letters do not shift; the edge may cut a letter in two for a frame. Per-letter edges stay possible later.
- **A crawl and a scroll move the whole quad** across the screen, which is the same picture as moving texture coordinates over a strip and needs no second texture. The strip is as wide as the chip's texture limit allows (`graphics-side`, at most 4096), as the design says, and a longer line is refused in the rasteriser's words: the request word `nofit 1` (new) makes the rasteriser refuse a text it would have made smaller.
- **Speed is a share of the screen a second** (2 to 100 percent of the height for a scroll, of the width for a crawl), not pixels; the time of a fade or a typing is `seconds`, and `out` is the fade out. A scroll or a crawl that has run off the screen is done until Replay (or it loops when Loop is on).
- **Opacity is baked in the picture** as in slice 2 (the rasteriser scales it), so the Opacity slider builds a picture again; the renderer also takes an `opacity` multiplier that nothing in the panel sets yet (slice 6, for OSC and MIDI, can use it without a rebuild).
- **The graphics are drawn at the screen's size, not at the final pass's.** Design 3.1 says to rasterise for the size the final pass draws at (720 lines while the display's scaler brings it up). Positions are fractions of the screen so the picture scales with the pass, but the text is not made sharper for 720; it is as soft as the masks and the codes are there. Not done; row GM8 looks at it.
- **Held means cleared.** While Access is on the screen the slots are cleared and the pictures stay built; they are loaded again when it goes, without a new picture. The pin screen calls `gfx.watch()` itself when a code is shown or hidden on request; a code that comes from a controller or the first-run screen is still noticed by the 2 second tick.
- **Fall back and come back.** The mode ("gpu" or "overlay") is read from the player (`Player.graphics_stage()`, from `pvj-render`'s `graphics` and `graphics-side`, version 0.11.0 on) on each pass and each tick. A change draws everything again the other way from the same picture files. Going to classic plays nothing again (the graphic is simply there, still); coming back to the renderer starts each graphic's motion as a show does, and so does a renderer that was restarted (the screen came from nothing).
- **Classic and lean steps are finite and bounded**: about 12 steps a second, a picture over 6 MB is shown at once, 12 MB of pictures at once, at most 30 s a motion, and the thread waits when nothing runs. The design's "refused with plain words" for scroll and crawl on these tiers stands; a motion chosen on another node (or before a fall back) is kept and drawn still, with the reason on the graphic.
- **Not in this slice**: keys and effects (slice 4), over or as a source (slice 5), the MIDI and OSC actions for position, opacity, line and replay and the state sent back (slice 6; only the panel's Replay and the "new line plays the motion again" exist), a measured frame cost (nothing was deployed; the numbers are for the device rows GM1 to GM15).
