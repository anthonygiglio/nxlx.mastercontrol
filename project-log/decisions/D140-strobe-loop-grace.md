## D140. A press at a loop point waits for the picture (2026-11-14)

At the start of each pass of a short loop, and for a moment after a seek, mpv has no video-params while the same item plays, so a put (the Strobe's included) was refused with 409 "Nothing with a picture is playing". Effects.put now calls _ride_out_gap before it takes its lock: when there is no video-params, the screen is not a generator's stand-in, the player is not idle, the same path is loaded, and mpv is seeking or at the start of the item (time-pos under PICTURE_START, 1 second, or not readable), it looks again up to PICTURE_LOOKS (5) times, PICTURE_LOOK_GAP (0.1 second) apart, on the request's own thread and outside the lock. It stops at once if the item, the epoch or the clears change or the player goes idle. Then the normal fresh picture() read decides, or refuses as before. Cost: a press landing in a gap waits up to half a second; nothing waits when there is a picture or no gap. Why this replaced a remembered picture (three review rounds): a remembered read needed a place to take it (a hook in play, play_to, playlist_step, mpv's own advance, play_source) and a look thread (a race with the lock, with the fake mpv in every server test, no cancel on a stop), and a stale or plain picture let unfit formats, streams with no frame and refused generator pairs through. Waiting for a real read has none of those: an unfit clip, a stream with no frame, a stop and a new item behave exactly as on master, because only a fresh read is ever used. Later rounds: the panel's toggle, Next, Previous, switch and preset (_ask) ride out the gap too, once per request; a put by hand that is overtaken during the wait (an Off, a newer put) gives up and answers superseded; the Strobe counts its leases from after a start (Strobe._count_from_now), so a wait at a loop point does not use up an OSC lease of half a second; the MIDI reader may block up to half a second in a gap, which its backstop lease of 10 seconds allows. The looks sleep through Effects._sleep, which tests replace.
