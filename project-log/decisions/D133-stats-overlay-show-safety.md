## D133. The stats overlay is the Owner's, off by default and never on by itself (2026-11-10)

Show stats on output draws text over the room's screen, so it follows rules that keep it from ever appearing in a show. (1) The state is `pvj/statsoverlay.py`'s memory and nothing else: not in settings, an export, a preset, a scene, the schedule, autostart, the Room or the fleet, and an import that names it is refused whole. (2) The one place it is turned on is the route handler `Api.set_stats_overlay` (`POST /api/stats-overlay`, role full, refused through the support tunnel); it is in neither `policy.LEGACY_LIVE` nor `GUEST`, and has no OSC address, so MIDI, OSC, DMX, the Room and a Guest cannot reach it. (3) At every start of pvj-web it sends `osd-overlay ID none`, because the player outlives the panel. (4) It goes off by itself when the player is another process (a crash, a screen mode, a renderer change or fall back; checked by pid at every sample, and at once by the renderer and screen routes), when the auto-off time runs out (15 minutes by default, 1 to 240 asked for, a new call re-arms it), and after five seconds of a player that does not answer. (5) It is taken off the screen while a PIN, join code or QR code is shown (`access_on_screen()`) and during a blackout, and comes back after if still on. (6) An event line says who turned it on and off, and why when it went off by itself. It draws in an id of its own, never with `show-text`, which the PIN and recovery codes use. Why: a debugging aid left on at a venue is a fault the audience sees, and a code hidden under it is a locked-out owner. Cost / rules out: a soak longer than four hours needs it turned on again; there is no setting to keep it on across a restart, on purpose.
