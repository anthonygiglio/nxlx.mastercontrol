## D114. Two malloc arenas for the player and its renderer (2026-10-31)

`install/pvj-player.service` sets `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`, so glibc keeps two malloc arenas for pvj-player, pvj-render and mpv instead of up to eight per core. Why: the six hour soak of 2026-10-31 found pvj-render's growth was all anonymous memory in regions the size of per-thread arenas, 360 MB to 900 MB, with GPU buffers, libraries and threads level. Run for 2 hours with the cap, under the same load and the same nine transitions: 396 MB at 2 hours and flat from 15 minutes, against 793 MB and rising at the same point. Cost / rules out: render CPU 294 to 297% against 294 to 299%, flips 59.9 to 60.1 a second against 59.8 to 60.0. Missed vblanks per 5 minute window were median 24, range 8 to 43, against median 18, range 5 to 59: about 0.13 against 0.10 percent of vblanks, the median a little worse, the worst window better, and 25 windows against 73, so neither a cost nor proof of none. Set in the unit, not with `mallopt` in the renderer, because mpv and the player share the service and it can be overridden from `/etc/pvj/pvj.env` without a build. What was not measured: which thread's allocations filled the arenas, more than 2 hours under the cap, and classic mpv under the cap.
