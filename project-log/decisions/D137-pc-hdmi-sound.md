## D137. On a PC node, Automatic sound is the HDMI or DisplayPort sound of the screen the picture is on, matched by ELD (2026-11-14)

What: on any node that is not a Pi, the sound output Automatic (`/api/audio`, `device: auto`) means the HDMI or DisplayPort sound device of the connector the picture is on, when it can be told, and mpv's own choice when it cannot. The connector is the one the player said it draws on (the screen report, D128), else the chosen output when connected, else the first connected HDMI screen, then DisplayPort. The connector is matched to a sound pin through the monitor name: the EDID in `/sys/class/drm/cardN-NAME/edid` and the ELD files `/proc/asound/cardN/eld#CODEC.PIN` carry the same name. Without a name, or with no single pin of that name, the only connected HDMI or DisplayPort screen is matched with the only pin that has a monitor present. The pin's PCM device is the one the driver names `HDMI k` for pin k (`pcmDp/info`), else HDA's fixed 3, 7, 8, 9 ... The mpv name used is `alsa/plughw:CARD=X,DEV=D` (the one measured), else `alsa/hdmi:CARD=X,DEV=k`, and only when mpv lists it. The periodic check (`ensure_audio`) and the player's restart after an output change (`apply_audio`) follow the picture. An output the Owner chose by hand is kept as it is. A Pi is unchanged: its vc4hdmi rule comes first, and the PC rule never runs on a Pi.

Why: on a PC the ALSA default is the analog jack, so Automatic sent the sound away from the screen. Measured on an Intel M710q (Debian 13, HD 630): a test tone on Automatic never reached the HDMI capture card on HDMI-A-1; on `alsa/plughw:CARD=PCH,DEV=3` it did. The monitor name is read, not guessed from port letters or pin order alone, because which DRM port a codec pin serves differs from board to board.

Cost / rules out: two screens of the same model, with no other clue, are not matched (Automatic stays mpv's own choice there; the Owner can choose by hand). A DP MST stream (`eld#C.P.D`, D above 0) is left out. On newer Intel graphics the HDA driver may give a pin the first free PCM instead of its own (dynamic PCM assignment), which the pin-order reading does not see; this is unproven on such a board. AMD and NVIDIA HDMI sound use the same ELD files and are expected to work the same way, unmeasured. The panel needs no change: it already shows `automatic_is`.
