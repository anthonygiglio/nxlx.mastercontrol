## D126. The node knows who is connected and what each last changed, in memory only (2026-11-07)

Anthony asked whether a node knows a controller is connected and whether people see the other active controllers. Until now `auth.last_seen` held a device's last request in memory but was never told to anyone, and the file kept a time at most once a day (for the idle expiry of a guest's device).

**Connected now** is a request in the last 30 seconds (`pvj/presence.py`). The panel asks for the status every second while it is visible, so an open panel is always in; a tab in the background drops out after about 30 seconds (browsers slow a hidden page's timers) and returns with its next request. The panel says so; it is a count of seconds since the last request, not a promise that someone looks. Every request through `Api.handle` marks its device; the node's own callers (MIDI, OSC, DMX, the Room) are not people and are not marked. A support login is marked as its own entry.

**What is told to whom** is in `GET /api/status` under `presence` (the panel already asks for it every second, and the reply is a few hundred bytes): the Owner sees every connected device by name and role, and a remote support session as "Remote support"; an Operator sees the names and roles of the Owner's and the Operators' devices and only a count of the Guests; a Guest sees a count and nothing else. A support login is told like an Operator, and sees itself. Never an address, a token or an id: an entry says only whether it is the asking device. The same rule decides the last ten changes: for each, the control area, whether it was this device, seconds ago, and who (name and role) only as the viewer may be told it; an Operator is told "a Guest" for a Guest's change, a Guest is told only the area and the time, and a support change is told to the Owner and to support itself.

**A change** is a request that went well and changed a control, by area (Playback, Mix, Pads, Vibes, Shaders, Effects, Mapping, Projectors, Room, Sound, Tempo, Renderer, Screen, Network, Schedule, Controllers, Sources, Media, Look, Modules, System). Sign-in, devices, codes, access, HTTPS, recovery and the support tunnel are never a change here and are not told. The last ten are kept, newest first.

**Memory only.** Nothing in this is written to the disk, the card or the settings file, and a restart of the panel empties it. Cost / rules out: it is not a history (see the event log, D125, for what happened), a device that has the panel open in a hidden tab shows as gone after 30 seconds, and the Guests are counted, not named, to an Operator, so an Operator cannot see which guest did what. The panel side (a line "Changed by iPhone 12 s ago" on a card) reads this reply.
