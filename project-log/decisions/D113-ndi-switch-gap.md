## D113. A switch between NDI sources keeps the old picture until the new one is ready, and does not wait for sound from a source that has none (2026-10-31)

The helper's open is two calls, `open` and `handover`, with the panel's `release_pipe` between them. The old receiver gives back its connection and keeps the pipe (`freeze`), so the player goes on showing the last picture while the new source connects. A failed press still ends black with the reason on the page, and a hand-over that never comes is given up after 10 s. A source that sent no sound in the whole `AUDIO_WAIT` is remembered for 10 minutes and waited for only 0.1 s the next time; late sound still ends the stream as "changed" and the source is forgotten as silent. Sound stays off by default and that is not changed. Why: the node showed nothing new for 0.83 s (median) after a press, and the old picture's early drop and 0.5 s of waiting for sound that never came were ours (D110 addendum). Cost / rules out: the old picture is frozen, not live, for as long as the new source takes to connect (up to the open's budget when a source is slow, then a failure shows as black); a source that was silent and then starts sound after the first 0.1 s of an open is missed once, and the panel opens it again with sound (one more switch time, once); the new connection waits up to a quarter of a second (at most a second) for the old one to be given back. Does not undo D62, D86, D87 or D110. Not shown on a node yet: whether mpv 0.40 holds a picture while its pipe stands still, and what the gap is now (`tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`, NS1 to NS6).
