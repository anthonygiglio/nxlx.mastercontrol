## Addendum to D129 (2026-11-08): what slice 2 changed from the design while it was built

Built as the design says (static text and logos on every tier through mpv, bundled fonts, Play > Graphics, OSC text), with these differences, each for a reason found in the code:

- **The helper opens one kind of file now: a font.** Slice 1's addendum and journal say it opens no file. Text needs the fonts, which are in the release (`pvj/fonts`), so the helper takes `--fonts DIR` (the unit passes `@PVJ_DIR@/pvj/fonts`) and opens `DIR/<name>.ttf` for a name of lower-case letters, digits and hyphens that the panel chose from its own list. Nothing typed or uploaded names a file, no font is sent to a browser, and the system is still read-only to the helper. (Fonts could have been sent in each request instead; 2 MB for Noto Sans each time was the worse trade.)
- **Roboto Condensed is OFL 1.1, not Apache 2.0.** The design said to re-read each licence from the release; this one differs from the design's table. All eleven fonts are SIL OFL 1.1. Their total is 5.4 MB, not 1.5 to 3 MB, because they are bundled unchanged (no subset: a subset of an OFL font with a Reserved Font Name would be a modified version). Orbitron and Press Start 2P declare Reserved Font Names; unchanged, they are fine.
- **Place is an anchor and a nudge.** The design's example (`y: 80` with `anchor: "bc"`) did not say what x and y mean. They are a nudge from the anchor (percent of the screen's width and height, down is positive), the anchor is one of nine points (an edge sits 5 percent in), and the picture is kept on the screen. A text's `scale` multiplies its size; its rotate and mirror are kept in the data and not drawn yet. Text size is a share of the screen's height, a logo's of its width, as the design says.
- **Outline and shadow are shares of the text's size**, so a preview at a third of the screen is the same picture. Outline width 1 to 20 percent, shadow softness 0 to 100.
- **The Library tab is a card on the Graphics page.** Slice 1's addendum left it "for the pull request that adds Play > Graphics". Upload, thumbnails, rename and delete are the Logo files card of that page, so the page does not send anyone elsewhere. The Library itself is unchanged.
- **Previews take the typed line.** `POST /api/graphics/preview.png` with `{id, text}` draws the line as typed and keeps it nowhere, so the preview is what the wall would get and nothing is sent to the wall until Send. The line travels in the body (changed after the review: it was first in the address, and so in the access log). The panel asks for one preview in 250 ms for all the graphics and the node limits them to 5 a second.
- **`("POST", "/api/graphics")` is in `policy.LEGACY_LIVE`.** The design's OSC addresses need a controller to reach it; OSC is held to that list, which is closed to new routes unless one is added on purpose. It is added on purpose and the role table (`tests/test_roles.py`) has its row ("live": the Operator, the Owner, a live support session and the box's own callers). `GET /api/graphics` is not in it: a controller reads nothing back from here. A Guest is refused every route, with guest controls open or locked.
- **The OSC addresses of this slice** are `/pvj/graphic/N` (switch over, or 0 and 1), `/text`, `/send` and `/pvj/graphic/hide`. Position, size, opacity, a saved line by number, replay and the state sent back are slice 6. `/pvj/graphic/N` is a small addition to the slice's list (text, send, hide): without it a pushed text could be shown by `send` but never hidden by number.
- **Settings are written at most twice a second.** A burst of OSC pushes is one write (the last text wins) and the worker flushes what is owed; stopping the engine flushes too.
- **The lean-tier spike answered no** (the journal has the numbers): mpv 0.41's `osd-overlay` draws an ASS event at one fixed time, so `\move`, `\fad` and `\t` do not run. The table of design section 3 stands. `tools/osd-ass-spike.py` repeats it on a node (row GT14). Fade and typewriter "stepped about 12 times a second" on classic and lean are slice 3, not this one.
- **Overlay stacking** is settled by the review's addendum (graphics 20 to 27, masks 30, codes 40 to 43).
- **What is not here yet** (said in the panel): motion, keys and effects, over and as a source, the MIDI actions, the Graphic pad, settings export and import, and the state sent back over OSC.
