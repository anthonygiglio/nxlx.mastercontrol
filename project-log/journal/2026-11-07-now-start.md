## 2026-11-07, the panel opens on Now (`now-start`)

- **Why.** The owner, 2026-11-07: "open on Now". This is follow-up (f) after Now (#214).
- **What.** When the page is first loaded, or the device has just been paired, an owner or an operator starts on Now. A guest starts where they did before: on Room while that module is on, or on Play otherwise. Nobody already on another screen is moved.
- **Tests.** Every check that read the first screen after a load now expects Now for an owner and an operator, then goes on to the screen it was testing:
  - panel.test.js: pairing, a recovery code, the plain-HTTP owner, the presenter with Room off, the Room's live device, Ambience;
  - shell.js roles();
  - sandbox.test.js;
  - signal-pages.js (scenes-presenter);
  - screenshots.js, whose pictures of Pads go there first.
- **Also in screenshots.js.** Every reload goes back to Pads before its picture. A reload lands on Now, and the "live" pictures and the preview step were taking pictures of Now and timing out.
- **Run on the HP's WSL:** tests/ui/sandbox.test.js, tests/ui/panel.test.js and tests/ui/screenshots.js all pass, each run alone.
- **Review of the PR.** A screen chosen by hand (go, or a page of Setup opened) ends the landing at once. A tap made before the node's modules were known is no longer taken back by Now on the next drawing. Landing on Now after a pairing again, in the same page, still happens. The sandbox test's comment says where each role lands.
