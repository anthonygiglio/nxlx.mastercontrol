## 2026-11-09, device tests on node-01 and node-02 (shader transitions, graphics motion and static, picture helper)

Nobody was paired and no PIN was typed, so every row that needs the panel as Owner or Operator is NOT RUN and says so in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`. What ran was driven at the renderer's socket (`pvj-shader`, `pvj-fade-to-program`, `pvj-crossfade`, `pvj-graphic`), at the node's own `pvj-raster` socket as root, at mpv's socket on node-02, and over ssh, with the screens watched through the capture cards.

Passed on both: GT1, GT3 at the helper, GT14 (mpv 0.40.0 says NO), RA1, RA7, FD1.
