## 2026-11-11, The Strobe button on the panel (D134)

- **Why.** The owner asked for "a hold-to-strobe button for the panel... that stops the moment you let go", with rate and colour controls. The node's side (PR 281) is merged and deployed; this is the panel's part, given to the panel session by the coordinator.
- **What.** A Strobe card at the top of Shape > Effect: "Hold to strobe", a big target (88 px tall), with a line saying Ready, Starting, Flashing, Waiting a moment or why it cannot, and the settings (Flashes a second up to the node's cap, Colour, How long each flash lasts, Flash to). It holds under a holder made for this tab, renews every 0.2 s, and lets go on anything that could mean the finger is gone (pointerup, pointercancel, pointerleave, blur of the button or the window, a hidden or left page) and on a refused renewal. Space and Enter hold too. Showing the card arms the node. Operators and Owners only, not over remote support, only with the Shaders module on. Not on the transport strip: a strip button is pressed in passing, and its fold order is fixed at every width; a strip copy can follow if the owner wants it.
- **Review of PR 282.** Every D134 rule confirmed. The test now waits for the picture (GET /api/effects says available) before the first press; it pressed too early on the CI runner and the hold was refused, as it should be. The release goes with fetch keepalive (so it is sent while the page is left); a hold that answers after the person let go is released again at once. On a touch screen "sliding off" ends the hold on pointerup (the browser keeps the touch on the button); with a mouse or a pen pointerleave ends it at once.
- **Checked.** tests/ui/panel.test.js against the sandbox node: armed when shown; a press of 0.75 s is one hold, renewals, one release; one holder of the form panel- and 24 hex digits; sliding off, the window losing focus and Space; a refused renewal stops it with "Let go and press again"; Waiting is said; the rate and the way are sent; the cap of 3; the button 56 px or more and fitting at 390, 820 and 1440. Not pressed by a person at a real strobe; not measured on a Pi.
