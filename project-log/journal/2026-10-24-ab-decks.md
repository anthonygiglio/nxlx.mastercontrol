## 2026-10-30, D108 revised with the owner's answers

- **Decided and written in:**
  - Surfaces gain a Program choice; Source 1 is deck A alone, Source 2 is deck B alone; a surface without a choice shows Program while Performance mode is on.
  - Level acts on each deck separately; Blackout covers everything once, at the end. The table in D108 says what that does in normal, add, screen and multiply. Under multiply the levels compound, and at the Centre a level of 0 on either deck makes the whole picture black. That is worked out, not yet seen on a screen.
  - Size, position and rotation stay per deck for now; transform controls for the mixed output are added, upstream of the surfaces' mapping.
  - The Fade button fades in either direction by the side the mix is nearer to; from the Centre it goes to B; the time is the whole fade; a press during a fade stops it.
- **Constraint added:** on the Pi 4 at 1080p with 4 sources in software decoding the output held 40.7 frames a second (30 October). The owner ordered plain playback smooth first, then transitions, then shaders and effects. Decks building waits for that and for the short Pi spike. Nothing is claimed about decks' performance.
