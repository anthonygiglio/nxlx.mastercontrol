## 2026-11-07, Presence: who is connected, and what each last changed (D126)

- `pvj/presence.py` keeps, in memory, the last request of each paired device and support login and the last ten changes by control area. `Api.handle` marks the device on every request and records a change after a request that went well. `GET /api/status` carries `presence`, shaped for the viewer's role: Owner everyone (support as "Remote support"), Operator names and roles of Owner and Operators plus a count of Guests, Guest a count only; no address, token or id.
- Reply shape: `presence: {window_seconds: 30, count, guests, devices: [{name, role, you, seconds_ago}], changes: [{area, by, role, you, seconds_ago}]}`; `by` and `role` are null where the viewer may not be told.
- Tests: the window, each role, the support case, areas and the cap of ten, the node's own callers, that the module never opens a file, and the status over the API. The panel side is the Panel design brief chat's.
- Review changes (decided by the coordinator, Anthony can overrule): an Operator is told an Owner's device by role only (as GET /api/devices hides it), a support login no device name at all, a Guest only `count` (`guests` 0); a scan, showing the OSC key and MIDI learn are not changes; a removed device or a factory reset drops entries and changes at once (`Auth.on_revoke`); uploads and the settings import mark presence and count as changes.
