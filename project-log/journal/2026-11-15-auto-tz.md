## 2026-11-15, automatic time zone for a node (J-129, D141)

- **Why:** the schedule runs on the node's local time. node-01 sat on UTC until 13 November, so an 8 pm entry would have fired at 2 pm. A fresh node comes up on whatever the image gives it (UTC).
- **Built:** `pvj/tz.py` (the strict name check, the zone in use, the list for a picker). pvj-sysd gets `set_timezone` and `timezone`: the name is checked (pattern, a real TZif file under /usr/share/zoneinfo, and `timedatectl list-timezones` where it can be asked), `timedatectl set-timezone <zone>` runs with that one name, and the zone is read back and compared. The panel's routes are `GET /api/timezone` and `POST /api/timezone` (Owner only; the POST is in `support.REMOTE_DENY`, so never through remote support). `POST /api/pair` takes the browser's `tz`: the first Owner pairing on a node still on UTC, with no confirmed zone, sets the node to it and the reply says so. The flag is `settings["timezone"] = {"confirmed": true, ...}` (read with defaults, no schema change; in `boxcare.BOX_OWN`, so never exported or imported).
- **Panel:** a bar for an Owner when the node's zone differs from the browser's ("This node is on X, your device is on Y. Use Y?", Use or Not now); the Schedule page shows the node's zone, its offset from this device, a warning, and an Owner's picker (this device's zone first). The scheduler status now reports `zone` (IANA) and `utc_offset_minutes` beside `timezone` (the short name), and the scheduler re-reads /etc/localtime at each check (`time.tzset()`), so a change takes effect without a restart.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_timezone.py` (name check, helper action with a fake timedatectl, the route, the roles, the first-pairing rules, the schedule report); the two role tables; a block in tests/ui/panel.test.js (the harness has no system helper, so the node's answers about its zone are given on their way, as the clock warning's test does).
- **Not verified:** nothing was run against a real node or a real timedated. Whether `timedatectl set-timezone` works from pvj-sysd's sandbox (ProtectSystem=strict; timedated writes /etc/localtime, not the helper) is expected from how set-time already works, not tried. Not tried across a real daylight-saving change.
- **Review fixes:** "Not now" posts `{keep: true}`; the first-pairing set needs no other Owner device and no schedule entry; `valid_zone` refuses `localtime`, `posixrules`, `Factory` and lowercase-only names; the helper fails closed on an empty or unreadable `list-timezones` and passes `--` before the zone; a failed set or an unsaved flag is said in the pairing reply and the route's answer; a tick() test across a zone change west and east.
