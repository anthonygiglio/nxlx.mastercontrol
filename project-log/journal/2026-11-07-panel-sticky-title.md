## 2026-11-07, the title bar stays at the top while a screen scrolls (`panel-sticky-title`, proposal 2 of pvj/PROPOSALS.md)

- **Why.** The owner, 2026-11-06, trying the Figma prototype: after scrolling down to the tabs and opening another screen, the title bar was out of view. In the panel it scrolled away with every long screen.
- **What.** The title bar (`#wshead`) sticks to the top of the window. What already stuck to the top now starts under it: the message line, the rail, the screen tabs, the Setup index, the mapping tools, the Library column, a split's side. The page measures the title bar's height into `--head` as it measures the strip's into `--dock` (the same ResizeObserver).
- **Where it does not.** The sweep's rule (`tests/ui/sweep.js`: the bars that stay put leave at least half of the window) failed in 16 cases with the title bar pinned everywhere: at 320 x 568 a Signal title bar, the strip and the tabs took 304 to 375 px, and with the strip opened with More even 390 x 844 went over half. So on a phone (under 600 px of panel) the title bar scrolls away when the window is under 800 px high, while the strip is opened with More, or when the title bar is taller than a quarter of the window (a long job line, large text); and under 480 px high at any width, as before. Tablets and laptops keep it at the top, More open or not.
- **Run on the HP's WSL:** `tests/ui/panel.test.js` with its width sweep passes; sweep.js and signal-pages.js pass. In the sandbox, scrolled down: the title at 0 and the tabs and rail under it at 390 x 844, 820 x 1180 and 1440 x 900; at 844 x 390 the title gone and the tabs at the top.
- **A lesson of the night:** two browser tests at once on this laptop failed each other on timing, and a sweep of leftover test players stopped the other run's. One at a time.
- **The review** (the coordinator's): the offline banner now shows above the pinned title bar (z-index 14, under Signal's floating message at 15 and the picker at 20); More unpins the title on a phone only; `scroll-padding` keeps what is scrolled to or focused clear of the pinned bars (WCAG 2.4.11), from `--head` and `--sub`, which the page measures only while they are pinned, with getBoundingClientRect and again when the window changes height. A case in the browser test: pinned after scrolling at 820 x 1180, still pinned with More open, the offline banner above it, and scrolling away at 390 x 700.
