## 2026-11-09, The black frames at clip loop points are in the clip

- The finding of the node-01 pass (2 to 4 black captured frames at every loop of "Lumia 1.mp4" with `loop-file=inf`) came from the clip: its last frame, 606 of 606, is pure black. mpv's `loop-file` seeks at each pass and the software decoder takes 150 to 300 ms to deliver the first new picture, so the renderer holds the last picture, the black one, meanwhile. The renderer draws nothing black of its own at a loop.
- How it was shown: a test-hooks counter (`pvj-test-blank`) that reads the middle of a video layer after each draw; a log of events and draws that put the one blank draw at the last frame, before the seek; a scratch headless build on node-01 (in `/tmp`, removed) that saw the same; and captures of the card on node-01 with clips of my own: no black frames over 6 loops for a clip with none, 5 dark frames a loop for the same clip with 2 black frames added, and for Lumia 3 and 4 dark frames by `loop-file` against 1 through a list (the panel's own loop, D118).
- Tried and dropped: holding the layer's picture when libmpv has none (skip-render and the frame info). libmpv's frame info never said "present" with the skip flag, so the layer would have stopped drawing; and there was no black draw of the renderer's to hold against. Nothing in the renderer or the player changed apart from the test counter.
- Left open for the owner: a black last frame in a clip is a content choice. If wanted, the list could cut it with an `outpoint` before the last frame (needs a look at the last frame when the list is made); not built.
- Tests: `LoopGapTest` (2) and one more in `LoopListTest`. Rows LG1 to LG4 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`. node-01 was idle before and is idle after; my files on it are removed.
