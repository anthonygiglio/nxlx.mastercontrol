## 2026-10-31, NDI switches: the old picture stays up, and no wait for sound that is not coming (D113)

- **The gap:** a switch from one NDI source to another showed nothing new for 0.83 s at the median (21 of 21 worked, 0.68 to 1.10 s; D110 and its addendum). Two parts of it were ours: the old picture was dropped before the new source was ready, and about 0.5 s was `AUDIO_WAIT`, waited out for sources that send no sound.
- **The change, part 1:** the helper's `open` is two calls. `open` (with `"split": true`, which the panel sends) connects to the new source and waits for its first picture while the old receiver gives back its connection and keeps the pipe (`Receiver.freeze`), so the player goes on showing the last picture. The panel then has the player let go (`release_pipe`, moved to after the open), and `handover` closes what was kept, sees the pipe without a reader and lets the new writer through. An open without `split` hands over at once, as it always did, so the helper's other tests and any script are unchanged.
- **A failed press** ends as it did: the helper closes everything it holds (so the player gets the end of its stream), the panel has the player let go (black screen), and the page says why. If the player does not let go when told, a new call (`letgo`) closes only the kept old stream and the player is asked once more; only if it still holds the pipe is the press refused (the page says so), as before. A new source the panel never hands over is given up by the helper after `HANDOVER_SECONDS` (10 s), with the old stream, so the old picture cannot stay frozen for ever.
- **The change, part 2:** `Service.no_sound` remembers a source that sent no sound in the whole `AUDIO_WAIT`; its next open waits `AUDIO_WAIT_SILENT` (0.1 s) for sound. Sources with sound are never made to wait (the wait ends at the first block). If sound turns up later under a picture opened without it, that ends the stream as "changed", as it always did, the source is forgotten as silent at once, and the panel opens it again with sound. The memory goes after `NO_SOUND_SECONDS` (10 minutes). With sound off (the default) there is no wait and no memory.
- **Tests (`tests/test_ndi_switch.py`):** 14, none skipped, plus four tests in `tests/test_ndi.py` changed to the new order (the pipe swap now happens after the hand-over; the player-does-not-let-go tests supply the new source's picture first). 12 of the 14 fail on master as it was (run by hand against a checkout of origin/master): the old stream ended before the new source was handed over, no `handover` call, no memory of silent sources, the player let go before the open. They hold the order of the calls, that a failed press leaves no kept stream, that a hand-over that never comes is given up, that a player that does not let go is not written over, and the wait times with sound and without.
- **Not shown:** anything on the node. The stand-in library and a stand-in player prove the order and the waits; whether mpv 0.40 keeps the last picture while a pipe stands still, and how much of the 0.83 s the rest (mpv letting go and loading) is, only `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md` rows NS1 to NS6 can say. A frozen old picture counts as "no new frame" for the dongle measurement, so the measured gap shrinks only by the sound wait; what the change removes is the black screen before the new source is ready. The old connection is given back (the helper waits up to a second for the threads inside the library) before the new one is asked for, so one connection at a time; that wait is spent while the old picture still stands.
