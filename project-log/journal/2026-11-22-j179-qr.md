## 2026-11-22, QR sticker for feedback without a login (J-179 slice B, D147 addendum)

- Built on the open slice A branch. A public page `GET /feedback` (three faces, optional words, Send, "Thanks, sent") and `POST /api/feedback/visitor`, off by default behind the Owner's switch "Let people without a login send feedback (QR sticker)" (`POST /api/feedback/open`, never through support, never from an import file). Notes are kept in the same store with the role "visitor", the node's context and no address.
- Limits for the route with no login: one note in 10 seconds and 10 in an hour for each address (IPv6 by /64), a bounded table of 512 addresses in memory, a 4 KB body, the CSRF header, a JSON type and the same origin; the node-wide caps of slice A apply, and a node-side refusal gives the sender his turn back.
- The Owner's card under Setup > Health got the switch and **Print the QR sticker** (`GET /api/feedback/sticker`, printed through the access sheet's print path). The QR code is made by the existing `mctl/qr.py`; no library was added.
- Added `tests/test_feedback_visitor.py` (the limiter, the switch, the page, the POST's requirements, the sticker's address, the encoder's data codewords) and rows to test_roles, test_carried_roles and test_support_reach.
- Not seen in a real browser or on a phone, and the sticker was not scanned with a camera or a decoder here (zbar is not installed): the printed look and a real scan are still to do.
