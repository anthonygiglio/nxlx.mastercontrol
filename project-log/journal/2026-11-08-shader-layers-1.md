## 2026-11-08, shader transitions slice 1: a generator on each video layer (renderer)

- Built slice 1 of design/shader-transitions_2026-11-08.md (D130, decided today): pvj-render 0.9.0. Source 1's two video layers each hold a generator (`gen[LAYERS]` in fx.c, a mailbox `app.gen[]` beside `app.fx`), so no existing slot number moved. A layer's picture is its generator's texture when it has one; `mix_pass` and `move_pass` now take textures. Slot 0 of source 1 on the `pvj-shader` socket command means the front layer's generator (the incoming one from the moment a fade starts); the property says `layer-generators`.
- Lifetimes: the IPC thread only asks (a pending program or a clear in the layer's mailbox). The render thread builds, draws and frees every GL object. The outgoing generator runs through the fade and is freed by the render thread in the turn the fade ends (`fx_layer_end`), or at the top of the next turn when a load, a stop or a new crossfade cuts the fade (`end_fade` asks, `fx_take` frees).
- Unchanged by design: the effects chain (slots 1 to 3), the level, the final pass, D73's cut (the panel is not changed and still never asks for a fade into or out of a shader), classic and the Pi 3. A renderer with no generator in either layer draws exactly what 0.8.0 drew.
- One behaviour moved at the renderer level only: a crossfade asked while a generator is on the front layer used to leave that generator over the whole fade; now it is the outgoing picture and is freed at the end (design section 2.2, slice 2 builds the rest). The panel cannot reach this before slice 3, because `fades_from_clip()` is false while a shader is loaded.
- Tests: LayerGeneratorTest (8) and ManyLayerFadesTest in tests/test_native_shaders.py, on Mesa's software GPU: the mix at the clock's share read back by its pixels, a wipe with a program on each side, a value reaching only the incoming program, both programs drawing through the fade (passes a second), a shader out to a clip, cut paths freeing the outgoing program, a refused program in a fade, and 160 fades (a third cut short) with flat descriptors, threads and program objects (PVJ_LAYER_FADES sets the count; the design's 2,000 is run by hand).
- Device rows LT1 to LT9 added to tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md, NOT YET RUN. Nothing was run on node-01.
- Needs an independent review of the C diff before slice 3 (render thread and GL object lifetimes), as the design says.
- Review fixes (#239): a clear asked by `end_fade` is no longer overwritten by a load that lands before the render thread's next turn (the load only adds to it; `take_slot` clears first, then builds), so a refused load cannot leave the old generator as the new front picture; a load whose layer is cut while it waits is now answered "the layer was ended before the load took effect" (`dropped` in the slot), not success; `app.level_resync` is written atomically. Tests: a cut then a refused load, repeated (PVJ_LOST_CLEAR_TRIES), and LayerLoadAnswerTest with two clients and a slow build. Slice 3 note on the refused set to slot 0 added to the design.
