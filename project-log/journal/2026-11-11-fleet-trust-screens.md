## 2026-11-11, Trusting a node and loading a fleet list from the panel (D132, after slice 5)

- **Why.** Slice 5 (PR 276) gave the panel the hand-over, but a pair of nodes could only come to trust each other from the command line: there was no screen for the fingerprint acceptance (design 3.4) or for loading a signed fleet list. The coordinator agreed this as the follow-up, with two Lows from the review of PR 276.
- **What.**
  - Setup > Nodes on this network, the Owner's card: the fleet list held, and Load a fleet list (fleet.json and fleet.sig chosen together and sent as they are, to `POST /api/fleet/list`). Trust a node by its fingerprint for each node on the network with a secure connection that is not trusted yet: its card's fingerprint (`POST /api/fleet/accepted/look`), a box for the fingerprint read on that node's own panel (never filled in, design 17 item 10), a choice of what its devices may do here, and Trust (`POST /api/fleet/accepted`). Stop trusting here for a node accepted by fingerprint (`POST /api/fleet/accepted/remove`; this node only, asked first).
  - The rows of the card put their buttons, and the fingerprint form, on a line of their own: with two buttons, the name was squeezed to one letter a line at 390 px.
  - Review Low (a): the TLS page's form-action names the trusted nodes only for a paired Operator or Owner. Low (b): the address last checked by certificate comes first, then at most 4 per node, a node at a time, before the 32 (`Peers.verified`, `Api.handover_forms`). Design section 22 item 8.
- **Review of PR 278.** CI failed the guard in tests/test_roles.py (a request gets its device in two places only): the page policy's lookup was a third. The HTML document is served without the gate, so there is no gate's device to reuse; the lookup is now one handler helper, `_paired_staff()`, that answers yes or no and hands no device to a route, and the guard counts three knowingly and checks that the third is there. The Low: the card asks for the nodes on the network when it opens and after a change of trust only, not after every press.
- **Checked.** tests/test_handover_panel.py (17: the policy only for paired staff on the real TLS listener, the checked address first, a flood for one id takes 4 places); tests/ui/panel.test.js (the list loaded and refused with one file, Stop trusting, Trust by fingerprint with the box empty and Trust waiting, the form at 390, 820 and 1440 with the name not squeezed).
