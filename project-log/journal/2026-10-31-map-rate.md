## 2026-10-31, shader values follow the beat on the New renderer; the clock and paused effect rows on the node

- **What.** The Sound page's shader mapping sent a value at most twice a second on every renderer, a limit written for classic mpv, which compiles each value. On the New renderer a value is only set (D106), so the limit now holds for classic alone and the New renderer's own shader gets up to 20 a second (`soundout.map_gap`). The smoothing's lowest setting goes from 0.5 s to 0.05 s, since 0.5 s flattened a beat into a swell. The default stays 1.5 s and saved settings stay valid.
- **Why.** BC2 on the node: with a tapped clock at 100 BPM the shader moved, but the picture pulsed at about 0.3 Hz, an alias of the beat.
- **Device rows recorded.** BC1, BC3 and PC1, PC2 passed; BC2, PC3 partly run; BC5 passed with sound off; BC4 not run. PC4 is not a fault: the 0.9 to 1.3 s is the mapping being built (0.6 s table and 0.3 s shader for 2 surfaces on a Pi 4), the same over a playing clip. The row's words are unchanged, its result says so.
- **Open.** The 20 a second path has not run on a node when this is written, nor its CPU cost with sound on. Rerun BC2 and cost it after the deploy. Nobody has looked at a beat on a real screen. The 1.5 s default still hides a beat, and a change to it is a UI matter for the rework.
