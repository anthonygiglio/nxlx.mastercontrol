## 2026-10-31, the box clock (D116)

- Built `pvj/clock.py`: a pure, standard-library flywheel with an injected time function. Sources in order: link (a slot, unfed), tap, osc, sound. A quiet source hands over to the next; the last good tempo keeps running and the status says whose it was and how old.
- Wired it to the existing sound path and no new page: `Sound.levels()` gives the clock's pulse, tempo and beat count to the meter, OSC out and the shader input while a tap or OSC steers, with or without a sound device. Sound is untouched otherwise. The levels file and pvj-render did not change.
- Routes `GET /api/clock`, `POST /api/clock`, `POST /api/clock/tap`. The action `clock_tap` is in the shared table (MIDI, OSC `/pvj/clock/tap`) and DMX has an optional tenth channel. A support session may look and not change.
- Tests: `tests/test_clock.py` (39 tests, fake clock, no sleeping) and three rows in `tests/test_roles.py`. Device rows BC1 to BC5 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md` are not run.
- Not done on purpose: Link, MIDI clock in, a new tracker, saving the tempo, any new card.
