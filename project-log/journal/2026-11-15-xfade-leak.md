## 2026-11-15, the New renderer's memory over crossfades (J-157)

- Found why pvj-render grew with every crossfade on node-03 (62 to 782 MB in 50 with software decoding, to 1,148 MB with vaapi-copy, 238 to 1,905 MB in an hour): not a lost decoder, texture or EGL image (GL objects, fences and threads stayed flat), but glibc's heap. Its threshold for mapped blocks rises with every large block freed, so frames and packets came from the two arenas and two decoders at once cut them up. In use followed the clip (113 MB at 1080p, 360 MB at 4K); free and held grew from 244 to 452 MB.
- Fix: pvj-render fixes M_MMAP_THRESHOLD at start (main.c, heap_setup); MALLOC_MMAP_THRESHOLD_ or glibc.malloc.mmap_threshold in GLIBC_TUNABLES still wins. New property `pvj-heap` (used, free, mapped, arena, threshold, trims). Test: HeapTest in tests/test_pvj_render.py.
- First measurement, 256 KiB, node-03, leak.sh 50 crossfades: software decoding 4K readings 405 to 416 MB, 1080p 193 to 197 MB; vaapi-copy 4K 264 to 306 MB, 294 MB at the end. One-hour soak.sh: the player's processes 215 MB at the start, 321 MB at the end (4K readings 536 then 563 MB); RssAnon 209 MB after the hour.
- Review of #296: blocks under the threshold still cut up the arenas (about 0.8 MB a fade at 720p, whose chroma planes are just under 256 KiB). Added malloc_trim on a thread of its own after a video layer's file ends (at most once a second, never the render thread), and measured 128 against 256 KiB, each with and without the trim. WSL, 40 crossfades at 720p, RSS from fade 10 to 40: 256 KiB 276 to 290 MB (79 MB free at the end), 256 with the trim 208 to 214 (81 free), 128 KiB 223 to 231 (20 free), 128 with the trim 211 to 216 (22 free); the longest trim 58 ms at 256 and 41 ms at 128. node-03, leak.sh 50 crossfades, software decoding, the 4K readings at 10, 30 and 50: 256 KiB 405, 409, 416 MB; 256 with the trim 401, 403, 401; 128 with the trim 400, 402, 407 (1080p readings 182 and 185). vaapi-copy with 128 and the trim: 248, 261, 259 MB, 247 at the end (256 without: 264, 306, 294). The longest trim on node-03 was 11 ms, 4 ms with vaapi-copy. Chose 128 KiB with the trim: the same RSS as 256 on node-03 within a few MB, and on the desk at 720p (what a Pi plays) a quarter of the free memory held. Missed vblanks over 24 crossfades on node-03: 1,579 of 12,876 flips with it, 1,581 of 12,877 on master.
- HeapTest now checks the mechanism too (threshold read back, frames in mapped blocks, trims done), clears MALLOC_MMAP_THRESHOLD_ and GLIBC_TUNABLES from its environment, and prints its margins. On the desk: a build with neither the threshold nor the trim fails (142 then 182 MB free, 30 MB mapped); this one passes (14 then 20 MB free, 148 MB mapped, 30 trims, the longest 18 ms).
- mallopt and the trim are glibc only (`#ifdef __GLIBC__`); mallinfo2 needs glibc 2.33, mallinfo before that (Debian 11). Not compiled on Debian 11 or musl here.
- Open: not measured on a Pi 4 (the coordinator measures memory and pacing on node-01 after the merge); no pacing measurement beyond node-03's missed vblanks above; the 128 KiB build was not soaked for an hour (the hour was the 256 KiB build without the trim). node-03 got master back afterwards, with its settings as they were (its pairing PIN rotated with the test pairing).
