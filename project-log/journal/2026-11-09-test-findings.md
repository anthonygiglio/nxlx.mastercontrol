## 2026-11-09, fixes from the hardware test pass (node-01 and node-02)

- Finding 1, shaders at 720 lines with the settings at 540 and Automatic on. Cause: not a stray 720. Automatic (D93, `LiveEngine.height_for`) gives each shader the lines of its row in the table that was measured on classic mpv with one shader at a time, and the table lets nxlx-silk, nxlx-tunnel and most of the others (41 of 46) go to 720. On the New renderer two programs draw during a fade, and two shaders at 720 lines hold about 41 flips a second on node-01 where 540 holds 60 (D130 was measured at 540). Fix: on a player that runs programs of its own (`Engine.native()`), Automatic is capped at the box's one height (`config()["height"]`, 540 on a Pi 4). A height chosen by hand still wins, classic keeps D93. Test: `AutoDetailTest.test_the_new_renderer_never_draws_above_the_boxs_height_under_automatic` (fails without the change: 720 != 540). pvj/SHADERS.md says it. Not changed: the table itself, and the program already on node-01 (it is rebuilt at 540 the next time a shader is shown).
- Finding 2, PT4: no `/run/pvj/player/renderer.json` on node-02. Intended, not a bug: `renderer.serve`, `fallback` and the switch write the report, and a player started with classic writes none (the docstring of `Renderer.drawing` says so); the panel reads its own board for the card when classic draws (`settings_profile`). The row was wrong, so PT4 now says a Pi 3B on classic has no report and the card shows lean, and the `profile` in the report is looked for on a Pi 4 on New.
- Finding 3: the comment in pvj/graphics.py said a graphic shown again during its fade out fades in from nothing; `gfx_show` (render/src/gfx.c) resumes from the level the fade out had reached. Comment fixed, no behaviour change.
- Rows: RA5 is 8192 x 4882 accepted and 8192 x 4883 refused; RA8's Good column now says what its flags give (ls /run/pvj lists names, an AF_INET socket is made under PrivateNetwork) and that the real unit is tighter (InaccessiblePaths for /run/pvj, RestrictAddressFamilies=AF_UNIX, no @network-io), read from install/pvj-raster@.service and pinned by tests/test_raster.py, not run in a sandbox; the unit needs no change. FD9 holds `-/boot/firmware` since D128.
- Tests run (WSL, software GPU): tests.test_shader_engine (78), test_shaders (93), test_graphics (65), test_raster (66, 34 skipped), test_units (54), test_renderer (74), test_profile (11). test_native_shaders runs 115 with 107 skipped here (no built renderer), so the New renderer's path was not run.
