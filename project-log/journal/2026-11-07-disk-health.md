## 2026-11-07, A Health line for free disk space

- `Health.storage()` (pvj/health.py) adds `storage` to the Health report: one row for each distinct filesystem under the media folder (`api.media_dir`) and the state folder (`paths.state_dir()`), told apart by `st_dev`, from `statvfs` (no program started; the answer is kept 10 s). Row: `{id, name, folders, state, text, free_bytes, total_bytes, free_percent}` with the same states as the other lines (`ok`, `warn`, `bad`, `unknown`) and plain text such as "Storage: 1.4 GB free on the media disk (5 percent): running low. Remove clips you no longer need, or move them to a bigger drive." The worst row counts toward `overall`. A folder that is missing or unreadable is a `warn` row that says so; a filesystem that reports no size is `unknown`. Nothing raises.
- Thresholds, for clips: a 1080p H.264 clip is about 75 to 150 MB a minute and ProRes several times that. Warn under 2 GB free, or under 10 percent while also under 20 GB free; bad under 500 MB, or under 3 percent while also under 2 GB. The percentage matters on a small card (16 GB at 10 percent is 1.6 GB) and not on a big drive (10 percent of 4 TB is 400 GB), which is why it only counts while the free space is also small. 1 GB is 10**9 bytes.
- Tests with a fake `statvfs` and `stat` cover the thresholds, one line for one disk and two for two, a missing folder, a size not reported, no media folder, the 10 second cache, and that no program is started.
