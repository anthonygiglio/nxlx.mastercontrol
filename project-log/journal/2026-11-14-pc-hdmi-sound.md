## 2026-11-14, On a PC node Automatic sound goes to the screen the picture is on, when that is beyond doubt (J-155, D137)

- **Why.** On the Lenovo M710q (Debian 13, Intel HD 630) the sound on Automatic went to the analog jack, mpv's own choice there, and never reached the HDMI capture card; `alsa/plughw:CARD=PCH,DEV=3` (the HDMI sound of HDMI-A-1) did, at -24 dB.
- **What.** `pvj/hardware.py`: `hdmi_sound_screens` (which HDMI PCMs have a screen, from ALSA's `HDMI/DP,pcm=N Jack` controls, with each PCM's ELD name, maker and product, read with `amixer -c N contents`), `parse_amixer`, `eld_identity`, `edid_identity` and `hdmi_sound_for`: one PCM with a screen is the screen's; with several, only a screen told apart from every other on both sides by name, maker and product. `pvj/api.py`: `_auto_audio` on a node that is not a Pi uses it for the screen the picture is on, as `alsa/plughw:CARD=X,DEV=N` or `alsa/hdmi:CARD=X,DEV=k`, only when mpv lists it; otherwise `auto`, as before. `GET /api/audio` gives `automatic_note` ("Several HDMI screens: choose the sound output by hand") and the panel's sound section shows it. A choice made by hand is kept; a Pi is unchanged. Decision D137.
- **Found on the node.** Two earlier versions were wrong on the real M710q with three screens: counting ELD files as ports (9 files, three a port for DP MST) gave only the first screen a device, and port order gave HDMI-A-2 PCM 7 where the tone test showed PCM 8. The ELDs and EDIDs also contradicted each other (D137), so names are trusted only where every screen is told apart.
- **Checked.** `tests.test_pc_sound` (14 new: amixer output and fake /proc/asound and /sys/class/drm trees, the measured Kaby Lake contradiction falling back, one projector, screens told apart, the API following the picture and its note, a choice by hand, no amixer, a Pi), with `tests.test_audio` and `tests.test_hardware` unchanged and passing (36 together); `node --check` on the panel. Not run as a whole on the M710q: deploying waits for the end of the show freeze. The panel's note is not checked in a browser.
