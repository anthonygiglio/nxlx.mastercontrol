## 2026-11-14, On a PC node Automatic sound goes to the screen the picture is on (J-155, D137)

- **Why.** On the Lenovo M710q (Debian 13, Intel HD 630) the sound on Automatic went to the analog jack, mpv's own choice there, and never reached the HDMI capture card; `alsa/plughw:CARD=PCH,DEV=3` (the HDMI sound of HDMI-A-1) did.
- **What.** `pvj/hardware.py`: `edid_monitor_name`, `hdmi_sound_pins` (the HDA ELD files with the monitor name, whether one is present, and the pin's PCM device) and `hdmi_sound_for` (a connector to its pin, by monitor name, else the only screen with the only present pin). `pvj/api.py`: `_auto_audio` on a node that is not a Pi uses the pin of the connector the picture is on (the screen report, else the chosen output, else the first connected HDMI, then DisplayPort), as `alsa/plughw:CARD=X,DEV=D` or `alsa/hdmi:CARD=X,DEV=k`, only when mpv lists it; otherwise `auto`, as before. A choice made by hand is kept; a Pi is unchanged. The periodic check and the restart after an output change follow the picture. Decision D137.
- **Checked.** `tests.test_pc_sound` (11 new: fake /proc/asound and /sys/class/drm trees, the API following the picture, a choice by hand, no mapping, a Pi), with `tests.test_audio` and `tests.test_hardware` unchanged and passing (33 together). Not run on the M710q or any PC node: deploying waits for the end of the show freeze. Unproven on hardware: the real ELD and EDID names matching, mpv listing the plughw name, and newer Intel boards with dynamic PCM assignment.
