## 2026-11-14, On a PC node Automatic sound goes to the screen the picture is on (J-155, D137)

- **Why.** On the Lenovo M710q (Debian 13, Intel HD 630) the sound on Automatic went to the analog jack, mpv's own choice there, and never reached the HDMI capture card; `alsa/plughw:CARD=PCH,DEV=3` (the HDMI sound of HDMI-A-1) did, at -24 dB.
- **What.** `pvj/hardware.py`: `edid_monitor_name`, `hdmi_sound_ports` (the HDA ELD files grouped by port, with the streams a port from the codec file, the monitor name, whether one is present, and the port's PCM device) and `hdmi_sound_for` (a connector to its port, by a monitor name only one screen has, else by port order when there are as many screens as ports with a monitor). `pvj/api.py`: `_auto_audio` on a node that is not a Pi uses the port of the connector the picture is on (the screen report, else the chosen output, else the first connected HDMI, then DisplayPort), as `alsa/plughw:CARD=X,DEV=D` or `alsa/hdmi:CARD=X,DEV=k`, only when mpv lists it; otherwise `auto`, as before. A choice made by hand is kept; a Pi is unchanged. The periodic check and the restart after an output change follow the picture. Decision D137.
- **Found on the node.** A read-only probe of the first version on the M710q with three screens: 9 ELD entries (eld#0.0 to eld#0.8, three a port for DP MST), monitors on 0, 3 and 6, two named "HDMI TO USB" and one "HD TO USB", and only PCMs 3, 7 and 8. Entries were counted as ports, so only HDMI-A-1 got a device. Now read by port, and repeated names fall back to port order (the assumption is written in D137).
- **Checked.** `tests.test_pc_sound` (13 new: fake /proc/asound and /sys/class/drm trees, the Kaby Lake 9-entry layout with and without the codec file, two screens of one model, the API following the picture, a choice by hand, no mapping, a Pi), with `tests.test_audio` and `tests.test_hardware` unchanged and passing (35 together). Not run as a whole on the M710q: deploying waits for the end of the show freeze. Unproven on hardware: the port order assumption on other boards, mpv listing the plughw names for ports 2 and 3, and dynamic PCM assignment on newer Intel boards.
