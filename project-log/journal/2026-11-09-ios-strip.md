## 2026-11-09, The strip on an iPhone after the keyboard or a picker

- **Why.** The owner, in the Home Screen app on his iPhone: "The scroll behaviour across the pages is broken". In his screenshots of Now and of Play (Pads, Library, Shaders), the strip and tabs sat in the middle of the screen with the page going on below them, each time with a text box, a dropdown or the Nodes list just used. Reopening the app put it right. His iPhone is up to date, so the coordinator asked for a guarded workaround rather than waiting on iOS.
- **What is known.** WebKit with an iPhone 13 profile (Playwright on Windows; WebKit does not run on this WSL) keeps the strip (`position: sticky; bottom: 0`) at the foot at every scroll position on every page tried. So it does not reproduce off the phone. Reports of iOS 26 describe this: after the keyboard or a picker goes, `visualViewport.offsetTop` (or its height) is not reset, and fixed and sticky bars are laid out against the wrong viewport until the page is laid out again.
- **What.** On iOS WebKit only (`IOS_WEBKIT`: an iPhone, iPad or iPod user agent, or a Mac one with touch), the panel waits 350 ms after a focusout, a change (a picker closing), a visual viewport resize or scroll, or a pageshow. Then, if nothing is being typed in, the page is not pinch-zoomed, and the viewport's offset or height still disagrees with the window, it nudges: a one-pixel scroll and back, the strip and title bar get a class for one frame (`transform: translateZ(0)`), and the heights are measured again. Each nudge is counted on the root (`data-vvfix`). Elsewhere none of this is set up.
- **Checked.**
  - A probe in WebKit with the iPhone 13 profile and a stand-in offset (`visualViewport.offsetTop` made to read 180 for a moment) after a focusout: one nudge, the strip at the foot afterwards. With the offset 0: no nudge. With a field focused: no nudge. In Chromium (not iOS): `data-vvfix` never appears.
  - tests/ui/panel.test.js checks the Chromium side: no nudge is ever counted.
  - Not checked: whether the nudge cures it on his phone. tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md has IOS1 to IOS5 for him.
