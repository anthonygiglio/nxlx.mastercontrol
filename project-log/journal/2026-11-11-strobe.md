## 2026-11-11, the Strobe: a flash you hold, with MIDI and OSC (node side)

- **Built (D134).** `pvj/effects.d/fx-strobe.fs` (rate, colour, flash length, flash to the colour, to black or the colour added); `pvj/strobe.py` (the hold: a lease for each holder, a watcher, the loaded copy in a chain slot of source 1 gated by its amount, a fallback that makes it the effect); `GET` and `POST /api/strobe`, `POST /api/strobe/hold`, `/renew`, `/release`; MIDI `strobe_hold` (a gate: held while the pad or button is down, the hub renews while the controller is read, a pulled cable or lost messages let go) and `strobe_rate`; OSC `/pvj/strobe` (1 and 0, the sending address is the holder) with `/rate`, `/colour`, `/flash`, `/way`. The panel button is the panel session's, on this API.
- **Roles.** The routes are "live" (Operator and Owner), the four hold routes are also a controller's (`policy.LEGACY_LIVE`, with a comment), none is a Guest's, remote support is refused hold, renew and the settings and may release (`support.REMOTE_DENY`). Rows in `tests/test_roles.py`, `tests/test_support_reach.py` and `tests/test_carried_roles.py` (SAME). No events: no live action (Blackout, effects) has one, and a hold would be five lines a second.
- **Library.** The filter is listed, flagged as flashing (`speed_max` 1, or 4 with the opt-in), not in the order Previous and Next step through (`effects.HOLD_ONLY`), and not measured on a Pi 4 (`effects.UNMEASURED`; the test that every bundled filter is measured now says so). Its MIDI actions are in the mapping page's lists (`app.js`).
- **Tests (`tests/test_strobe.py`, 59 tests locally).** The state machine against stand-ins and a clock the test moves: the fast path is one value (no put, no effect call), release is one value, the lease ends within 0.5 seconds of the watcher (and a real thread does it in 0.5 to 0.75 seconds), renewals keep it, a renewal of a lapsed hold is refused, the 60 second ceiling, counted holders, a gentle stop that fails takes the copy off and a stop that cannot is tried again. The cap: no rate above 3 without the opt-in, 12 with it as 3 times a speed; a change of rate or flash length ends the stretch and the next starts a gap later; press, release, press cannot start inside the gap; 30 rounds of 400 random steps never start two stretches closer than the gap. The routes with the real API (classic path: the strobe is the effect and what was on comes back). MIDI through the mapper and through the hub with pipes (note on and off, a pulled cable, lost messages, MIDI switched off, the lease alone). OSC through the parser and the real receiver (1 and 0, the sender as holder, the lockdown layers in front, stopping the listener lets go). The filter on pvj-render built from `render/` (a hold on the real renderer is a value with no new file; colour and way with no new file; a Stop of source 1 and a new hold; **a rate of 99 handed straight to the renderer flashes at most 3 a second**).
- **Break-checks.** The filter's `clamp(rate, 0.0, 3.0)` changed to 99: the GPU test counted 36 flashes in 4 seconds against the 14 it allows, and failed. The gap test and the 60 second test were run first as failures (a float edge in the first, which was the test's, and a lease that was marked "spent" after plain silence, which was the code's, fixed).
- **Changed on the way.** `tests/test_effects.py` (the filter is the 13th of the project's own, moves, and is the one bundled filter with no Pi 4 row), `tests/test_midi_layouts.py` (the rate is a level with a shape).
- **Not done, not verified.** Nothing ran on a board, with a projector, or with a person at a button. The hold's latency on a Pi 4 is `started_ms` in `GET /api/strobe`, unmeasured (rows ST1 to ST8 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`). No real MIDI controller or TouchOSC layout was used. The settings are in memory, not saved. A stop waits for the player's own lock if a clip is being loaded.
