## 2026-11-11, the Strobe: a flash you hold, with MIDI and OSC (node side)

- **Built (D134).** `pvj/effects.d/fx-strobe.fs` (rate, colour, flash length, flash to the colour, to black or the colour added); `pvj/strobe.py` (the hold: a lease for each holder, a watcher, the loaded copy in a chain slot of source 1 gated by its amount, a fallback that makes it the effect); `GET` and `POST /api/strobe`, `POST /api/strobe/hold`, `/renew`, `/release`; MIDI `strobe_hold` (a gate: held while the pad or button is down, the hub renews while the controller is read, a pulled cable or lost messages let go) and `strobe_rate`; OSC `/pvj/strobe` (1 and 0, the sending address is the holder) with `/rate`, `/colour`, `/flash`, `/way`. The panel button is the panel session's, on this API.
- **Roles.** The routes are "live" (Operator and Owner), the four hold routes are also a controller's (`policy.LEGACY_LIVE`, with a comment), none is a Guest's, remote support is refused hold, renew and the settings and may release (`support.REMOTE_DENY`). Rows in `tests/test_roles.py`, `tests/test_support_reach.py` and `tests/test_carried_roles.py` (SAME). No events: no live action (Blackout, effects) has one, and a hold would be five lines a second.
- **Library.** The filter is listed, flagged as flashing (`speed_max` 1, or 4 with the opt-in), not in the order Previous and Next step through (`effects.HOLD_ONLY`), and not measured on a Pi 4 (`effects.UNMEASURED`; the test that every bundled filter is measured now says so). Its MIDI actions are in the mapping page's lists (`app.js`).
- **Tests (`tests/test_strobe.py`, 60 tests locally).** The state machine against stand-ins and a clock the test moves: the fast path is one value (no put, no effect call), release is one value, the lease ends within 0.5 seconds of the watcher (and a real thread does it in 0.5 to 0.75 seconds), renewals keep it, a renewal of a lapsed hold is refused, the 60 second ceiling, counted holders, a gentle stop that fails takes the copy off and a stop that cannot is tried again. The cap: no rate above 3 without the opt-in, 12 with it as 3 times a speed; a change of rate or flash length ends the stretch and the next starts a gap later; press, release, press cannot start inside the gap; 30 rounds of 400 random steps never start two stretches closer than the gap. The routes with the real API (classic path: the strobe is the effect and what was on comes back). MIDI through the mapper and through the hub with pipes (note on and off, a pulled cable, lost messages, MIDI switched off, the lease alone). OSC through the parser and the real receiver (1 and 0, the sender as holder, the lockdown layers in front, stopping the listener lets go). The filter on pvj-render built from `render/` (a hold on the real renderer is a value with no new file; colour and way with no new file; a Stop of source 1 and a new hold; **a rate of 99 handed straight to the renderer flashes at most 3 a second**).
- **Break-checks.** The filter's two caps (`clamp(rate, 0.0, 3.0)` and `min(rate, 3.0)` in the place in the cycle) changed to 99: the GPU test counted 36 flashes in 4 seconds against the 14 it allows, and failed. The gap test and the 60 second test were run first as failures (a float edge in the first, which was the test's, and a lease that was marked "spent" after plain silence, which was the code's, fixed).
- **Changed on the way.** `pvj/server.py` does not write a journal line for each 200 ms renewal that is answered 200. `tests/test_effects.py` (the filter is the 13th of the project's own, moves, and is the one bundled filter with no Pi 4 row), `tests/test_midi_layouts.py` (the rate is a level with a shape).
- **What CI found (first run).** The licence test counted the project's own filters (12, now 13), the panel test the same, and the GPU test that every bundled filter changes the picture could not judge a filter that flashes: its clip loops, so TIME runs and a flash is on or off by chance, and a flash of one flat colour is not a "picture" to `still()`. That test now judges a flashing filter (`FLASHING`) with the clip paused, by a sweep of 21 rates with the colour added to the picture: some rate must flash, every draw must be the picture or the flash, and amount 0 the picture again. I could not run that GPU test here (no virtual display); CI is its first run. The unit job of Python 3.9, part 1, timed out three times at 20m47s to 21m0s. It runs near its 20 minute limit on master too (master's own 3.12 part 1 timed out on 2026-11-08 and 2026-11-11), and the cause is in `tools/ci-shard.py`: its table counts every module it does not list as 3 seconds, and seven are not small (test_ticket 136, test_peer 136, test_cluster_remove 110, test_ticket_http 102, test_lease 100, test_sandbox 44, test_carried_roles 30; times from the 3.9 job's log, which stamps each test). Adding one more small module (test_strobe) moved test_ticket, 136 seconds, into part 1 and test_ticket_http out of it. The table now lists them and test_strobe (35), and the parts come out 840, 839 and 839 seconds by the table, where master's part 1 ran 19 minutes and parts 2 and 3 about 13. Seen in CI: the six unit jobs then ran 14 to 15 minutes each, and every check passed.
- **Owner's decision, 2026-11-11, "OSC strobe must repeat while held".** An OSC lease is the panel's 0.5 seconds; the sender repeats `/pvj/strobe 1` about every 0.2 seconds; one press with no repeats lasts at most about half a second. MIDI keeps the hub's renewal, and its 10 second lease stays as a backstop for a hub that stopped. OSC.md says how to do it in TouchOSC.
- **The independent review (head 2de825b), all fixed.** High: a restart of pvj-web while the strobe was lit left the player flashing with nobody holding it; at start-up (`Strobe.reset_player`, on a thread from server.build, with a back-off until the player answers) any slot of source 1 running the strobe's program, or its text in mpv's list on classic, is taken off (tests: the real renderer with the slot lit, a fake player that answers late, classic). Medium: MIDI's release and renewals bypass the hub's 50 a second limiter, a release that failed is asked again directly, and a renewal the node cannot take (not 409) lets go; the playback Speed above 1 divides the rate sent (the filter's TIME follows the clip's frames), and a change of Speed while held is a change of timing, D134 corrected; a renderer fallback mid-hold is a finished stop, not a wedge, and the stop's log is once in ten seconds. Low: `arm: false` while lit changes nothing and a release never clears a slot of its own that is not there; the panel must send a holder for each tab (SHADERS.md); lost messages clear a CC-mapped strobe pad's pressed state.
- **Re-verification, two gaps in `reset_player`.** It also scans the panel's own effect slot (slot 1), where the strobe sits when both chain slots are taken, and it no longer returns early when a hold has already been made: it runs once and leaves alone only the slot and route this run itself holds. A test for each.
- **Not done, not verified.** Nothing ran on a board, with a projector, or with a person at a button. The hold's latency on a Pi 4 is `started_ms` in `GET /api/strobe`, unmeasured (rows ST1 to ST8 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`). No real MIDI controller or TouchOSC layout was used. The settings are in memory, not saved. A stop waits for the player's own lock if a clip is being loaded.
