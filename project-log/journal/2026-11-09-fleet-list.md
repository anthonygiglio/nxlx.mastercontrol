## 2026-11-09, Node hand-over slice 1: the fleet list and its tool

- **Why.** D132 (design/node-handover_2026-11-09.md, section 3.3 and the slice table): nodes trust each other only through a list you sign with your root. Slice 1 is the list, the way to make and sign it on your computer, and the check a node will make. It changes nothing on a node and nothing reads a list yet.
- **What.** `tools/boxcert.py fleet new, add, remove, clear, sign, show, verify`. `pvj/fleetlist.py` is the format and the check, standard library plus the system `openssl`, and the tool loads that same file so the two cannot read a list differently. It parses strictly (unknown or repeated fields, repeated ids, keys or names, bad carry values and anything over 64 KiB are refused even when signed), verifies the root's signature over `nxlx-fleet-v1` + fleet id + serial + the file's bytes, and enforces the fleet id and the serial (a lower serial is refused; the same serial with other contents is refused too). `key` is the SHA-256 of the DER public key info of the node's certificate. docs/HTTPS.md has a section in plain words.
- **Choices the design left open.** The signature is the root key's SHA-256 signature, base64 in `fleet.sig`. `--carry` has no default so a level is always chosen. The working copy is `fleet-draft.json` beside the root; `sign` raises the serial itself. Two nodes cannot share a key or a name on one list. A key that changed for a node already on the list needs `--replace-key`.
- **Left out on purpose.** `GET /api/fleet/card` on the node is not added: it changes a node, and it touches the route policy tests (`policy.NO_GATE` is asserted to hold the probe alone in two places). The tool takes `--card` or `--cert` until it exists, and `fleetlist.make_card` and `parse_card` are ready for it. Nodes do not load lists (slice 2).
- **Checked.** tests/test_fleetlist.py (signing with plain openssl, not the tool: tampering at sampled bytes and every field that matters, another root, another fleet, lower and equal serials, a missing or wrong prefix, repeated ids, keys and names, bad carry values, malformed shapes, the size limit, the key hash against openssl's own) and tests/test_boxcert_fleet.py (the commands end to end with a throwaway root, a wrong passphrase writing nothing, the card asked over a local TLS server). Not tried: macOS LibreSSL and Windows OpenSSL.
