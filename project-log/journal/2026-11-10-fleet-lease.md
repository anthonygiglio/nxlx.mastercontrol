## 2026-11-10, node handover slice 4b: the lease, the poke, the 24 hour lapse and the sweep (D132)

- Built `pvj/lease.py`. When node A redeems a ticket it also gives node B a lease handle (128 random bits). A keeps only its hash with the device record (`lent`), B keeps the handle and a stamp (`lease`: when A last answered by the wall clock and whether it was trusted, by the monotonic clock, and the boot id) with its `via: node` record, so both survive a restart. About every 10 minutes B asks A, in one call per home node, whether each carried device is still paired and with what role. A good answer renews; "gone" or a Guest signs the device out; a lower role, or a lower carry at B, lowers the record; a higher role is ignored (`Auth.update_node_device` can only lower). A refusal, an answer not understood and silence renew nothing.
- The poke: when a device A had lent is removed (`revoke`, `revoke_all`), A pokes each node in the background (3 seconds apart per node); B reacts only by asking A, at most every 10 seconds per node. Both have allowances of their own (12 pokes a minute per approved node, 30 lease questions a minute per approved node, 5 failures a minute per source address for anything not approved) and touch none of the throttles that tickets, redeem and the peer channel use; a test shows junk from outsiders fills only its own address and leaves those throttles empty.
- The lapse: 24 hours after the home node last answered a carried device is signed out, not before. The wall clock is used only while it is trusted (and was when stamped); otherwise the monotonic clock inside the same boot. After a restart on an untrusted clock the age cannot be told: 10 minutes of grace, then the 24 hours start again (design section 20, item 5, for review). The sweep (no network, every tick and at once when the list, an acceptance or the deny list changes) signs out everything when the "Accept devices from other nodes" switch is off, everything from a node that is no longer approved (a deny always wins), lowers records above the carry, and ends lapsed ones. A record from 4a with no stamp starts its 24 hours at the first sweep.
- Tests: `tests/test_lease.py` (two real nodes, fake clocks): renewal, lapse at exactly 24 hours on both clocks, demotion lowering and promotion ignored, removal by poke and by the check when the poke cannot arrive, home node off leaving the lease to run, deny, switch, a list without the node and a removed acceptance ending everything at once, restart keeping leases at both ends, the allowances, and no handle in any event, log line, answer or the home node's settings.
- Left for 4c and 4d: the inverse role test and the route walk with a carried Owner, "Remove a node from this cluster" and the deny epochs. Not deployed.

## 2026-11-10, review fixes for slice 4b (PR 271)

- H1: `_wait` overwrote A's lease handle with the confirm handle, so every Switch hand-over was signed out by the first check, and the confirm answer carried the stored value. The confirm handle has its own name now. Test: Switch, then a lease check that renews, with no lease handle in the answers.
- M2: `ok` is never rewritten without an answer from the home node. After a restart the age cannot be told (an empty boot id counts as that too); a reachable home node renews at the first tick, and one that is silent at the end of the 10 minute grace means the device is signed out, with one event line. Design section 20 says so.
- M3: lease checks and pokes charge the failure budget of their target only, never the node-wide outbound count (`budget=False`). Checks started by pokes are capped at 2 a minute for all nodes at B; A sends at most 20 pokes a minute (the rest wait for its next tick). Tests: a node that is unreachable at four addresses and keeps poking leaves the node-wide count empty and a hand-over still works; pacing of a mass removal.
- L4, L7: a check merges its stamp under the settings lock and skips a record whose handle changed while it ran (a re-open is not undone; sign-out is conditional on the handle too), and saves once for all the devices. L6: the check uses `approved(node, key)` for the key the connection showed. The "was lowered" event is written only for a change that was applied.
- Second review: a check that fails is now tried again at the next tick (30 seconds) instead of ten minutes later, so the 10 minute grace after a restart is 10 minutes of trying; a record a home node renewed in this process is exempt from the "age cannot be told" sign-out even with no boot id; the "did not answer" line no longer promises 24 hours after a restart. Tests: a first check that fails and a later one inside the grace that renews; no boot id with a record just renewed.
