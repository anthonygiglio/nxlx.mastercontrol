## 2026-11-07, Now: what is on and what needs you, first (`panel-now`)

- **Why.** The owner, 2026-11-06: Mastercontrol is "a control centre that VJs can use", not an instrument. He approved Now as a new area ahead of Play, Shape, Room and Setup (design/panel-direction_2026-11-06.md; the Figma frames "00 ..." at 390, 820 and 1440). 2026-11-07: "Then build the now page."
- **What.** A new area, first in the rail and the phone's tabs, for an owner or an operator (a guest has none: a device that only watches lands where it did). One screen:
  - **Needs you**, across the top: every row of Setup that says Problem or Check, as the Setup index words it, with Open to its page (Back there says "Back to Now" and comes back here);
  - **On the screens**: Source 1 as the strip says it, with the place in the clip and the transition (every second), each further source of the New renderer, and Fade out, Next clip, Stop and Test pattern;
  - **The room** (with the Room module): its scenes as buttons, one line per projector, All on, All off (which asks first), and Open the walls;
  - **The node**: power, temperature, the player, the load and the address, from Health;
  - **Today**: People and codes, Schedule, Remote support and Updates, each as its row of Setup says.
- **Layout.** Four columns on a laptop, two on a tablet, one on a phone, over the whole width (the screen's usual 1100 px limit is lifted for Now). A row keeps its state and its button at the right of its words.
- **How it reads the node.** Health, sources and the room every 5 s; the rows of Setup every 30 s, through the same answers the index uses. A look waits while a press is down (held) and an answer to a Now that was left is dropped (its own element).
- **Not yet.** Now is first in the menus but the panel still opens on Play (the start screen is a separate small change, so the panel's tests move one step at a time). "What happened" under Today waits for a feed of the node's events.
- **Test.** panel.test.js, on the owner's page after the projector warning: Now is the first area; it has the screens, the node and today; Needs you lists Projectors with its state and counts; Open goes to Projectors and Back to Now returns.
- **What the full run found, and what changed for it.**
  - tests/ui/shell.js knows the new area: the rail and the tabs start with Now for an owner and an operator, and their screens start with now/now; a guest has neither.
  - The test that feeds long names found a long scene name running out of a button. The scenes are now the Room's own blocks, as many to a row as have 140 px, with the name cut between words.
  - The Projectors test from #207 crashed the run once ("Route is already handled"): an answer still on its way when the route was taken off. Its handler now lets such an answer go.
