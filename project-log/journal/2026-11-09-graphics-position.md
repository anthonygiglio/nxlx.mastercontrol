## 2026-11-09, Graphics: the place selector after a crawl (a fix on slice 3)

- Anthony found on both nodes that after something like a crawl the position selector "doesn't align properly". First tried to reproduce a stale offset: the engine and the real renderer, headless, with the real rasteriser at 1080p and 720p proportions and with the stand-in blocks, every one of the nine places landed exactly where `graphics.position` says, fresh and after a crawl, a scroll, a fade and a typewriter, while a crawl or scroll ran, and after Replay. So nothing keeps an offset in the slot (`gfx.c` set and show), in the saved settings, in the preview or in the classic overlay path (same x and y as the GPU path).
- **Cause 1, a lost request in the engine's worker (`pvj/graphics.py`).** `process()` took a copy of the work to do at the start and, when it finished, removed that copy from the work set. A change made for the same graphic while the pass ran (a tap on a place just after a motion change, which also changes the picture's key because a crawl is made as one wide line) added the same ids, and the removal took them out again: the new picture was never built and the graphic stayed at its old place until some later change. It showed once in about eight runs of the new pixel test. The work set now counts additions; a pass removes its copy only if nothing was added meanwhile, and the worker looks again at once when work is owed.
- **Cause 2, a moving graphic shows nothing at the new place.** A scroll or crawl that has run off the screen (not looped) is nothing on the screen, so a tap on a place showed no change. And a crawl ignores the grid's column and the nudge across (it crosses the whole screen) and a scroll ignores the row and the nudge down, with nothing said. A change of place now plays a scroll or a crawl across again, the panel says which half of the selector counts, and the nudge that does nothing is greyed out.
- Tests: `tests/test_graphics_position.py` (new: pixel placement of every place at 320x180 and 1280x720 on the real renderer, fresh and after a crawl, a scroll, a fade and a typewriter, while they run, and after Replay; the lost request; the replay on a change of place; the classic path's x and y), `tests/ui/panel.test.js` (the selector shows the stored place after each motion change; the notes and the greyed nudges).
- Not measured on a node: nothing was shown on node-01 or node-02 (Anthony is using them). Rows GP1 to GP4 are in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`, NOT YET RUN.
