## 2026-11-09, Node hand-over slice 2: a node keeps the fleet list and knows who is approved

- **Why.** D132, slice 2: the node side of the fleet list, before any node talks to another. Storage, the card, the Owner's routes, the switch and the one question the later slices ask. Nothing is deployed and no node calls another.
- **What.** `pvj/handover.py`: the signed list stored beside `tls/` (checked against the pinned root, this node's fleet, a serial that only goes up, and the list must name this node with its current key), acceptance of one node by a fingerprint typed back, the deny file (storage), the switch `fleet.accept` (off, Owner only, in the box-own `fleet` section), and `approved(id, key)` with `carried_role`. `GET /api/fleet/card` (second route in `policy.NO_GATE`, TLS only, no secret), and under `/api/fleet` the Owner's routes (status, list, accepted, accepted/remove, accept), over https only and never through a support session. New event kind `handover`. A factory reset removes the files. The discovery switch no longer overwrites the other keys of the `fleet` section. The tool's message for a missing half of the signed pair now says what to do with no backup (the nit from the slice 1 re-check).
- **Left out on purpose.** Panel screens (the panel session owns app.js; its `EVENT_KINDS` needs a label for `handover`), tickets and the peer channel (slice 3), the Remove action and the lease. A list near 64 KiB cannot be posted (the API body limit is 64 KiB); real lists are a few KiB.
- **Checked.** tests/test_handover.py (the list, the root, the node's own id and key, serials, a crash between renames, acceptances, the deny file, the approved question row by row) and tests/test_handover_api.py (on a real TLS listener: the card, who may use each route and where, the switch kept, not exported and not importable, and a walk of every GET, the diagnostics file, a settings export and import and the logs for the list, the acceptances and the deny file). `test_roles`, `test_support_reach` and `test_https` hold the new rows and the two-route `NO_GATE`.
