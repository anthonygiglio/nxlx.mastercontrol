## 2026-11-01, the seamless loop of plain clips (D118)

- Built `pvj/looplist.py` (the lists and the one translation layer), `probe.exact`, `paths.loops_dir`, `renderer.loop_lists`, and in the renderer `end`, the queue's keys on every load path and the restart guard. PR #193. Unit tests in `tests/test_looplist.py`, and `LoopListTest` in `tests/test_pvj_render.py` runs the real Player against the real renderer, headless (built and run in WSL, libmpv 2.5).
- The audit of who reads what. Through `Player.ipc.request`, so all translated: `player.status` (path, position, duration, the loops, playlist-pos and count) and with it `api._public_player_status`, `_ndi_on_screen`, `_nothing_on_screen`, `_render_before`, `boxstate`, `oscfeedback` (time, length, progress), `health`, `pinscreen`, `vibes`; `sources_state` and `api` sources (the rows of pvj-sources); `playlist_files`; `sync.py` (`state()`'s file, place, length and loop, `apply_wall`, the client's `playing`, `position` and its `absolute+exact` seek, whose `target %= duration` now works on the clip's length); `transitions.py` (`_mark_frozen`, `tidy`, `_first_frame`); `screenmode._keep_words`; the carrier checks in `shaders.py`, `shaderlive.py`, `effects.py`, which only ever compare with `av://`. Seen as the player says it, through `Ipc.ask`, on purpose: `_wait_for_path` and `play_to`'s wait (a clip going over into its own list would otherwise seem there at once), the prune, `Player.loop`. `percent-pos` has no reader today and is translated anyway. No other position property is read anywhere in `pvj/`, and pad cues are render node cues, which never touch this.
- The audit of the funnel. `loadfile` appears only in `player.py`. `_play`, `_crossfade` and `play_to` take every local file through `expand_media`; `play_pipe` loads pipes, `play_source` an `av://` carrier, streams and test patterns are URLs. `probe.probe` and `overlay.py`'s convert open a user file in their own short mpv for a look or one picture and show nothing on the screen; they do not pass this funnel and were left as they are.
- Node facts, plain mpv in /tmp, null outputs, nothing installed touched: every line of D118 about BOM, ID3, HLS, loopback streams, `end`, the per-file queue (on for the list, back to off and 50 after the next load), a deleted inner file and wrong durations. A relative seek past the start of the first pass clamps to 0, and in a later pass it goes back into the pass before: the loop's own wrap.
- Found: `end` set plainly stays for the next file, and a gone inner file with loop-file=inf spins for ever. Both are in D118, both changed the design, and both are held by tests on the real renderer.
- Not run: anything on the node's own renderer. No seam number here is new; those are the two measurements'. See rows LL1 to LL10 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md`.
