## 2026-11-08, Graphics 2: text and logos on screen (slice 2 of D129)

- Built slice 2 of the design on `graphics-static`: a text or a logo is drawn still, over the screen, on every tier, with mpv's `overlay-add` (ids 20 to 27, the order of the list). The first thing Anthony can use on any node, a Pi 3 included. Motion, keys, effects and "over or as a source" are not here: the settings read them as none, the routes refuse them in words, and the panel says they come later and has no control for them.
- **The helper draws.** `render/raster/gfx.inc.c` (part of pvj-raster, one more request `RASTER1 GFX`): a line of text with FreeType (layout left to right with the font's kerning, a letter the font lacks from Noto Sans, one nobody has drawn as a box and counted; outline by distance to the ink so corners stay round, shadow by a blurred copy, a back plate; the text made smaller to fit the screen, never cut), or a stored logo scaled (averaged going down, bilinear going up), turned, mirrored and faded. One small premultiplied picture each, cut to the ink. The text is always after the one line that ends the settings, so a typed line cannot set anything. The helper reads a font from the folder `--fonts` names by a name of lower-case letters, digits and hyphens, and no other file.
- **Eleven fonts bundled, unchanged, all SIL OFL 1.1, each read from its release** (google/fonts at the commit the panel's two fonts came from): Archivo, JetBrains Mono, Inter, Bebas Neue, Oswald, Anton, Montserrat, Orbitron, Press Start 2P, Roboto Condensed, Noto Sans. Roboto Condensed is OFL 1.1 in its release, not Apache 2.0 as the design expected; recorded as found. Orbitron and Press Start 2P declare a Reserved Font Name (bundled unchanged, so it is not reused). 5.4 MB in all (the design said 1.5 to 3 MB; the variable files are the releases' own and none was cut down). Licence texts, checksums and REUSE.toml entries are in; a test pins them.
- `pvj/graphics.py`: the settings key `graphics` (no schema change), cleaning of typed text, the fonts, the maths of place (a nine-point anchor, a 5 percent edge, nudges), and a worker that builds one picture per change (newest wins, 5 a second for a graphic and 20 a second in all, the pictures of all that is on at most 48 MB, a refusal not tried again for the same picture) and tells the player. Routes (Operator and Owner only): `GET`/`POST /api/graphics`, `GET /api/graphics/fonts`, `GET /api/graphics/preview.png`; `GET /api/status` gives every role `graphics_on`. A Guest reaches none of it.
- **OSC**: `/pvj/graphic/N`, `/text`, `/send`, `/pvj/graphic/hide`. For this `("POST", "/api/graphics")` is the one route added to `policy.LEGACY_LIVE` on purpose (the list was frozen, but OSC is held to it, and the design wants a phone to push a line). OSC.md says plainly that with OSC on and no lock any device on the network can put words on the wall. The settings are written at most twice a second however fast it pushes.
- **Panel**: Play > Graphics (a page of Setup that is a screen of Play, as Mapping is of Shape): Hide all, Add text, Add logo, a card per graphic (the big Show or Hide, the line and Send, a preview the node makes of the line as typed, settings in a fold, saved lines), and a Logo files card (upload, thumbnails, rename, delete). The Library tab of the design is this card, so the page is self-contained.
- **The spike** (design section 3): does `osd-overlay` advance the ASS clock? Run through libmpv's software renderer with mpv 0.41 on a PC: no. `\move`, `\fad` and `\t` are drawn at their first moment every time, so scroll, crawl and true fades do not go to the lean tier this way and the table of the design stands. `tools/osd-ass-spike.py` is the program; a Pi 3's own mpv is row GT14. A second spike, whether a higher overlay id is drawn over a lower one, could not be settled here (libmpv's software renderer added the two overlays instead of laying one over the other, in both orders): row GT7.
- Checked here: `tests/test_graphics.py` (new: the cleaning, the stored shape, the changes and refusals, place, the routes for every role, the worker, previews, OSC, the fonts' licences and the build files), `tests/test_raster.py` (the real helper's text and logo jobs against the real fonts; PVJ_RASTER_TEST=1), the role table, units, install and license modules, and the browser test (`tests/ui/panel.test.js`, the Graphics step, the row and the menus; the page is in the width sweep and the Signal checks). The browser test uses a stand-in for the helper (`tests/ui/raster-stand-in.py`) and a screen of 1920 x 1080 that takes what is drawn, as the harness's player draws to no screen. Not run on a Pi, on the X27, under the real socket-activated unit, against a real projector or by anyone else: rows GT1 to GT16 in `tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md` are NOT YET RUN.
