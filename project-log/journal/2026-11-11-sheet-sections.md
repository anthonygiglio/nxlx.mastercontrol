## 2026-11-11, The Nodes sheet: this node first, and real section headings

- **Why.** The owner, on his iPhone with a marked-up screenshot: "Move the details about the active node (the node the user is currently viewing) to the top of the Nodes panel. Also, section headers like 'this node' are not clearly defining their sections." The headings were the small grey label style above a rule, so they read as part of the rows.
- **What.** The sheet's order is This node, Nodes on this network, This device, Tools. Each is a `section.nssec` with its own heading `h3.nshd`: the Signal title style (20/24, 700, full text colour) on a full-width band of the card tone, space before each section, and no rule between a heading and its first row. In both looks, at every width. The Nodes list keeps its `#nodespop` (now a div; the section around it has the heading).
- **The trust choices said plainly.** The owner, of the three choices under Trust a node by its fingerprint: "What does this section mean? I don't understand". They were roles of "its devices". Now a question, "When someone from nxlx-friend opens this node:", answered by "They keep their role (an Owner there is an Owner here)", "They can run the show but not change settings (Operator at most)" and "They need this node's PIN"; the button says Trust nxlx-friend, and the trusted rows say the same in a sentence.
- **Everywhere.** The answers wrap (one sentence each; at 390 px they ran past the card, clipped so the page did not widen, which the width check could not see: a check that every choice fits inside the card now). The Owner card's own sections (Fleet list, Trusted nodes, Trust a node by its fingerprint, Removed) get headings of the same title style, with no rule under them.
- **Figma.** The kit has no frame of the Nodes sheet (it was built after the kit's screens), so nothing there to change; asked the coordinator whether to draw it.
- **Checked.** tests/ui/panel.test.js: the order of the four sections, the first heading This node, the heading at least 18 px and bold, its band of its own tone with space before the section, no rule under it. Screenshots at 390, 820 and 1440.
