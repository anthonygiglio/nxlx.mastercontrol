## 2026-11-14, the waits in the panel test that never waited

- tests/ui/panel.test.js had nine `page.waitForFunction(async () => ...)` waits on the graphics card, and 37 more written `waitForFunction(() => fetch(...).then(...))` (one more in tests/ui/signal-pages.js). Playwright polls a function until it returns something truthy; a Promise is truthy at once, so every one of them was over on its first look.
- tests/ui/poll.js has `pollFor(page, check, timeout, arg)`: one page.evaluate per poll, 100 ms apart, each raced against the time left (a fetch that never answers no longer outlasts the timeout), and on timeout it throws with the check's text and the last answer. All 47 waits use it, with their own timeouts. (It is not called `until`: panel.test.js has two local helpers of that name, and the first spelling of this change was shadowed by one of them.)
- tests/test_ui_waits.py reads each tests/ui/*.js whole, blanks the comments, cuts out the first argument of every waitForFunction by matching brackets, and fails when it holds async, await, fetch(, .then( or Promise, or is a name whose definition in the file does. Its own cases cover multi-line calls, a comment before the call and a predicate passed by name.
- Two of the old waits turned out to guard nothing:
  - The projection mapping drag (the corner must move more than 100): on a phone the canvas sits above the top of the window (y of -352), so the mouse drag went nowhere. The test now scrolls the canvas into view first.
  - The shader speed slider at its top: the box holds 1.991 of 2 (the slider's top step), not 2. The wait now allows one step of the slider's range.
- Run locally (WSL, Playwright 1.56.1, node 22, `--unhandled-rejections=warn`; a full run takes about 42 minutes here): the whole test passed once with every wait really waiting. Master (not the branch) died in its own way: at a "Route is already handled" unhandled rejection in the route handlers near lines 480 and 2564, and with the flag at the strobe `steady` check (position 0.36 s); the branch hit the same two in some runs, so both come before this change.
