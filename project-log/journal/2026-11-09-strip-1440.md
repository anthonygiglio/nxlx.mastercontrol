## 2026-11-09, The whole strip at 1440 on Windows too

- **Why.** PROPOSALS 8: Chrome on Windows draws a classic 15 px scrollbar, so a 1440 window gives the panel 1425 px. The strip's last line (back and forward 10 s, and no More) was at 1440, so Windows kept More where a Mac at 1440 showed every control.
- **What.** That line is at 1400 now: back and forward 10 s are on the strip and More is gone from 1400 px of panel, with the tighter spacing from 1400 to 1639. The other widths (D72) are unchanged. PANEL.md says so.
- **Checked.** tests/ui/shell.js measures the strip at 1399, 1400, 1425 and 1440 (one line, inside the window, room left for what plays, More only under 1400).
