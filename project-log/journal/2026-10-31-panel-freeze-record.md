## 2026-10-31, A panel that died during a crossfade no longer leaves the clip frozen

- The "open, recorded and not fixed" note in DECISIONS (a panel that dies between freezing the outgoing clip and loading the new one leaves the player frozen until the next play) is closed by D112. The old note stays where it is.
- When a crossfade, wipe or slide freezes a clip that was playing, the panel first writes `transition-frozen.json` in its runtime folder (the player's process number and the file it plays; written by a rename). The record goes when the new clip has loaded, when the play fails, or when the hold thaws the clip. A panel that starts finds a record, removes it, and unfreezes the clip only if it is still the same player process, still paused, and still playing the same file. A clip the operator froze before the play never gets a record, so it is never touched. A freeze the operator makes after the new clip loaded is not touched either, even if the record was left behind, because the file differs or, with the same file, it is the one case that cannot be told apart (a record left behind between the load and its removal, a tiny gap, and the same file replayed).
- Tests in `tests/test_transitions.py` (class `FrozenByAPanelThatDied`): a panel killed during the still leaves a record before the freeze and the next panel thaws; a good hold, a failed play and an abandoned hold leave nothing; an operator's earlier or later freeze is left alone; a restarted player, a different file, a clip not paused, garbage or a link in place of the record, and an unreachable player all end with the record removed and nothing thawed. The whole of `tests.test_transitions`, `test_leftovers` and `test_lockrank` passes (287 tests). The tests use the suite's stand-in player; no real mpv or Pi 4 was run.
- Not proved here: that the real player answers the `path` and `pid` questions the same way after a real crash, on classic mpv and on the New renderer (device test X28 added).
