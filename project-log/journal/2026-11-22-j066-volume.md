## 2026-11-22, projector volume steps (J-066 alpha, D146)

- Built volume up and down for projectors: `PJLink.volume` sends `%2SVOL 1` and `%2SVOL 0` (the command now takes a class), `volume_up` and `volume_down` on `/api/projector`, the same two on `/api/room/group` (sent at once to each projector of the group, answering which did not take it), two buttons on each group card of the Room screen, and `/mctl/group/<n>/volume_up` and `/volume_down` in OSC. A projector that said class 1, or answers ERR1, ERR2 or ERRA, or is Epson-control-only, gets the words "This projector does not take volume from the network". No level, steps only.
- Permissions: the same routes as mute (operator and above); the guest policy refuses both routes, tested in test_roles and in the room and projector tests.
- Tests: the fake projector got a class 2 mode (`klass="2"`, `svol` to force an answer); new tests for the bytes sent (with and without the password digest), each refusal, a class 1 projector sent nothing, several projectors with their own answers, roles, the journal line, the OSC table. Existing tests unchanged except the fake's answer path.
- Not proven: the X27 and X39 taking SVOL, or the step size, or the sound reaching anywhere. Not built: the Projectors page's own buttons (the Room screen only), Epson ESC/VP volume, MIDI and TouchOSC (no projector table).
