## 2026-11-10, Opening another node signed in: the panel (D132, slice 5)

- **Why.** The owner, 2026-11-09: a device paired on one node, and allowed to see the others, "should be able to just open the other nodes without having to pair with each one". The server slices (1 to 4d) are merged and on both nodes; the owner said "Yes, start it" to this slice through the coordinator.
- **What.**
  - The Nodes list: a node this node trusts says "Opens signed in, without its PIN", and Open asks for a one-time ticket and posts the form to that node in a tab of its own with no opener (design section 5). The ticket and the secret are never in an address. A refusal sends the tab to the node's own address (its PIN screen) and says why. A node that joined after the panel was loaded is stopped by the page's policy; the line says to reload. Other nodes keep their plain link and say they ask for their PIN. A device that came from another node gets no Open (nothing chains). The note under the list is rewritten.
  - The server: `GET /api/nodes` marks each row `carry` and says whether this device may carry and why (`handover`); the check that a device came from another node is `Tickets.carried`, inside ticket.py as the source guard of tests/test_carried_roles.py requires. The HTML page's policy over TLS names the trusted nodes' https origins in form-action (`Api.handover_forms`, `page_csp(origin, forms)`): from what discovery already knows (`Browser.known`, which never starts a scan), private addresses only, each of the one shape CSP_ORIGIN allows, 32 at most.
  - Setup > Nodes on this network, the Owner's card "Nodes that open each other": the banner, deny_problem and the list's problem said plainly, the switch "Accept devices from other nodes" (Stop asked first), this node's fingerprint, the trusted nodes with Remove from this cluster (asked first, the count said), the removals with Allow again (the fingerprint typed back; a 409 and a 404 said plainly) and Allow again everything one node removed.
  - Design section 22 gains item 7, the known limit of a peer that pins a key at MAX_EPOCH - 2. tools/DEVICE-TESTING.md gains "Hand-over between nodes" with HO6 first.
- **Not done here.** Accepting a node by its fingerprint and loading a fleet list from the panel (the routes exist; the brief for this slice did not include their screens). Whether Safari takes the cookie from the cross-site form (HO6) is not known until it is tried on a real iPhone.
- **A test fixed on the way.** The Freeze check of the slow press (from PR 261) read the button before the next status had turned it to Resume, so on this machine it skipped the press back and timed out; it now waits for Resume. It failed the same way twice here and passes now.
- **Checked.** tests/test_handover_panel.py (the policy's shape and its 32, only trusted nodes at private addresses, the `carry` marks, the device that may not or came from another node, no scan from a page load); tests/ui/panel.test.js with a pair of nodes answered in the test (one ticket, one form, the secret's hash, no address with the ticket, no opener, the refusal, the stopped form, the Owner's card at 390, 820 and 1440).
