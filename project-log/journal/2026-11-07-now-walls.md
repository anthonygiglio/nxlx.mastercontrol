## 2026-11-07, The room on Now has the walls (`now-walls`)

- **Why.** Follow-up (d) after Now (#214), from the owner's direction: The room shows "the walls", not only a button to them.
- **What.** With the Room module on and walls set up, each wall that has projectors is a line on Now:
  - its name and the Room's own words for its state ("On · Matrix", "Some on, some off", "Warming up", a projector with no answer);
  - a chip for that state;
  - On and Off for an operator or the Owner, sent as the Walls screen sends them.
- All on, All off (which asks first) and Open the walls stay. A node with no walls, or without the Room module, lists its projectors as before. A projector's own problem stays in Needs you.
- **Test.** panel.test.js, once the main wall is on with its source chosen: on Now the wall says it is on, has On and Off, and On is sent and said.
- **Run on the HP's WSL:** tests/ui/panel.test.js passes, run alone.
- **Review of the PR.**
  - A wall's Off asks first, as All off and the Projectors screen do, and the On and Off pair waits while one is on its way.
  - On, Off, All on and All off are for an owner or an operator in the room, not a support login below full (which the node refuses).
  - The test also opens Off's question and answers Not now (nothing is sent), and routes a room with no walls (the projectors are listed).
  - A Guest has no Now (tests/ui/shell.js, roles).
  - pvj/PANEL.md has a section for Now.
